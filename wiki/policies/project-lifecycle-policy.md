# Overview
The Project Lifecycle Policy governs the entry requirements, maturity progression, and periodic evaluation of technical initiatives hosted within the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF)[^evt-technical-committee-pr-9]. Enacted by the [Technical Committee](../governance/technical-committee.md), the policy establishes objective criteria for evaluating project health, open governance adoption, and ecosystem utility[^evt-technical-committee-pr-9].

Day-to-day intake execution and committee review procedures for prospective projects are specified in the [Project Proposal Process](../policies/project-proposal-process.md)[^evt-technical-committee-pr-9].

# Architecture / Specification
The lifecycle model delineates progressive maturity tiers:

- **Sandbox**: Entry stage for exploratory, experimental, or newly formed open-source initiatives seeking foundational alignment with AAIF technical scope and open governance standards[^evt-technical-committee-pr-9].
- **Growth**: Stage for projects demonstrating measurable production adoption, diverse multi-organizational contribution, active release velocity, and adherence to security guidelines[^evt-technical-committee-pr-9].
- **Impact**: Highest maturity tier for mission-critical, widely deployed industry standards with broad institutional backing and self-sustaining governance.
- **Emeritus**: Terminal phase for completed or superseded projects transitioned into archival maintenance.
