# Overview

The AAIF Project Lifecycle Policy provides the formal framework through which open technical projects join, advance within, and retire from the Agentic AI Foundation [^evt-technical-committee-file-8f732085cd4f-04c1d0b2]. Enforced by the [Technical Committee](../governance/technical-committee.md), the policy establishes objective expectations, resource support, and governance alignment across distinct maturity stages [^evt-technical-committee-file-8f732085cd4f-04c1d0b2].

Intake into the lifecycle is governed by the [Project Proposal Process](project-proposal-process.md), requiring public documentation of OSI permissive licensing, code repositories, release practices, automated validation, and leadership governance [^evt-technical-committee-file-8f732085cd4f-04c1d0b2][^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-15a00e3a].

# Architecture / Specification

## Lifecycle Stages

The policy organizes projects into four distinct tiers [^evt-technical-committee-file-8f732085cd4f-04c1d0b2]:

- **Sandbox Stage**: Entry-level stage intended for nascent, experimental, or emerging projects. Focuses on fostering initial adoption, contributor growth, and alignment with AAIF architectural standards [^evt-technical-committee-file-8f732085cd4f-04c1d0b2].
- **Growth Stage**: Intermediate tier for projects demonstrating sustained development velocity, broadening multi-organization contributor bases, and verified production deployments [^evt-technical-committee-file-8f732085cd4f-04c1d0b2].
- **Impact Stage**: Climax maturity stage for mission-critical, industry-standard technologies demonstrating widespread multi-vendor adoption, rigorous open governance, and deep cross-project integration [^evt-technical-committee-file-8f732085cd4f-04c1d0b2].
- **Emeritus Stage**: End-of-life classification for projects that have completed their active utility, transitioned to alternative homes, or ceased maintenance, preserving historical artifacts under Linux Foundation stewardship [^evt-technical-committee-file-8f732085cd4f-04c1d0b2].

## Annual Review

All admitted projects undergo a mandatory Annual Review conducted by the TC to evaluate ongoing community health, governance compliance, milestone completion, and eligibility for stage graduation or retirement [^evt-technical-committee-file-8f732085cd4f-04c1d0b2].
