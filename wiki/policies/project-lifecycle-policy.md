# Overview
The AAIF Project Lifecycle Policy defines the official maturity tiers, progression milestones, and governance expectations for open-source initiatives hosted by the Agentic AI Foundation[^evt-technical-committee-pr-9]. It establishes clear entry gates, operational requirements, and graduation criteria administered by the [Technical Committee](../governance/technical-committee.md) through the [Project Proposal Process](../policies/project-proposal-process.md)[^evt-technical-committee-pr-9].

Projects advance through defined lifecycle stages based on technical maturity, community diversity, open governance compliance, and production adoption[^evt-technical-committee-pr-9].

# Architecture / Specification

## Lifecycle Stages
The policy defines graduated levels of maturity[^evt-technical-committee-pr-9]:
- **Sandbox**: Entry stage for early-stage, experimental, or emerging open-source technologies aligned with the foundation's mission. Provides a collaborative home under open governance with minimal adoption prerequisites.
- **Incubating / Growth**: Maturing projects demonstrating active development, multi-organization contribution, stable release cadences, and documented ecosystem evaluation.
- **Graduated**: Highest maturity stage reserved for foundational, production-proven technologies exhibiting robust multi-vendor governance, stringent security practices, and sustained adoption.

## Stage Transitions and Governance
Stage progression requires formal review and voting by the Technical Committee[^evt-technical-committee-pr-9]:
- **Admission**: New projects enter at either Sandbox or Growth depending on existing community traction and code maturity.
- **Growth Criteria**: Stricter criteria require demonstrated neutral governance, cross-vendor maintainer distribution, and documented real-world adoption.
- **Annual Review**: Hosted projects undergo recurring reviews by the Technical Committee to ensure ongoing alignment, healthy maintenance, and progression readiness.
