# Overview

The Project Lifecycle Policy defines the official maturity stages, criteria, and operational reviews governing open-source technical projects hosted by the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF)[^evt-technical-committee-file-29954f63e09f-b0f27f04]. Authoritative maintenance and enforcement of the policy is delegated by the AAIF Governing Board to the [Technical Committee](../governance/technical-committee.md)[^evt-technical-committee-file-29954f63e09f-b0f27f04].

# Architecture / Specification

## Governance Authority and Scope

Under Section 2 of the Technical Committee Charter, the TC implements, maintains, and enforces the Project Lifecycle Policy, subject to initial Governing Board approval[^evt-technical-committee-file-29954f63e09f-b0f27f04]:

- **Project Evaluation**: The TC reviews candidate project proposals against AAIF mission scope, technical merit, and intellectual property requirements[^evt-technical-committee-file-29954f63e09f-b0f27f04].
- **Lifecycle Decisions**: The TC adjudicates stage advancements and status changes across project maturity tiers—including Sandbox, Growth, and Impact stages[^evt-technical-committee-file-29954f63e09f-b0f27f04][^evt-technical-committee-file-b33563055168-8f2953e0].
- **Governing Board Recommendations**: The TC advises the Governing Board on accepting new Technical Projects and prioritizing Foundation resources to support active projects[^evt-technical-committee-file-29954f63e09f-b0f27f04].
