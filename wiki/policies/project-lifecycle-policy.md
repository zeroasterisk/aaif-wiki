# Overview
The Project Lifecycle Policy is the governing authority defining the lifecycle stages, admission criteria, graduation thresholds, and annual review procedures for technical projects hosted by the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF)[^evt-technical-committee-file-8f732085cd4f-04c1d0b2]. It establishes structured maturity levels that allow projects to participate, graduate, and receive mentorship and foundation support aligned with their operational maturity[^evt-technical-committee-file-8f732085cd4f-04c1d0b2].

# Lifecycle Stages
The policy defines four progressive stages for hosted projects[^evt-technical-committee-file-8f732085cd4f-04c1d0b2]:
- **Sandbox**: Entry stage for early-stage or experimental initiatives that align with the AAIF mission, designed to incubate innovative agentic capabilities and foster initial open-source community traction[^evt-technical-committee-file-8f732085cd4f-04c1d0b2].
- **Growth**: Stage for maturing projects with multi-organizational contributor diversity, established governance practices, regular release cadences, and measurable production adoption[^evt-technical-committee-file-8f732085cd4f-04c1d0b2].
- **Impact**: Flagship stage for mission-critical, production-proven agentic technologies demonstrating sustained ecosystem reliance, high-velocity maintenance, and resilient governance[^evt-technical-committee-file-8f732085cd4f-04c1d0b2].
- **Emeritus**: Terminal stage for inactive or retired projects that have concluded active technical development and transitioned into maintenance or archival status[^evt-technical-committee-file-8f732085cd4f-04c1d0b2].

# Governance and Progression
Proposals for project admission or stage progression are submitted via the [Project Proposal Process](./project-proposal-process.md) through standardized criteria covering permissive licensing, repository infrastructure, and leadership transparency[^evt-technical-committee-file-8f732085cd4f-04c1d0b2][^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-15a00e3a]. The [Technical Committee](../governance/technical-committee.md) conducts formal annual reviews for all active projects to evaluate adherence to stage expectations, community health, and graduation eligibility[^evt-technical-committee-file-8f732085cd4f-04c1d0b2].
