# Overview
The Agentic AI Foundation publishes member-driven case studies highlighting the adoption and measurable impact of open agentic AI standards such as the Model Context Protocol (MCP), goose, and AGENTS.md[^evt-foundation-file-d803a4dfa3ec-79586e70].

# Architecture / Specification
Submissions are accepted in two formats[^evt-foundation-file-d803a4dfa3ec-79586e70]:
- **Written Case Study**: Authored by member organizations or featuring customer implementations, detailing technical challenges, solutions, and measurable impact.
- **Webinar Case Study**: Recorded 20–30 minute presentations with Q&A hosted across AAIF distribution channels.

Case studies are prioritized based on demonstrations of agentic maturity, open interoperability, or quantitative operational acceleration[^evt-foundation-file-d803a4dfa3ec-79586e70].
