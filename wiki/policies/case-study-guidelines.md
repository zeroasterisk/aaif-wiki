# Overview

The Agentic AI Foundation Case Study Guidelines define criteria and submission formats for member organizations showcasing production implementations of open agentic AI standards, such as MCP, goose, and AGENTS.md[^evt-foundation-file-d803a4dfa3ec-79586e70]. The program provides reference implementations to the community while documenting tangible business impacts[^evt-foundation-file-d803a4dfa3ec-79586e70].

# Participation and Formats

Member organizations can participate through two mechanisms[^evt-foundation-file-d803a4dfa3ec-79586e70]:
- **Written Case Studies**: Articles detailing customer or internal deployments, architectural approaches, and quantifiable outcomes.
- **Webinar Case Studies**: Recorded 20–30 minute presentations with Q&A hosted across AAIF publication channels.

Case studies are prioritized based on demonstrations of agentic maturity, open interoperability via protocol standards, and measurable operational acceleration[^evt-foundation-file-d803a4dfa3ec-79586e70].
