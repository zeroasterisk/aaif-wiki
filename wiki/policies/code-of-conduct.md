# Overview
The Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) Code of Conduct establishes community behavioral standards, expected interpersonal norms, and enforcement procedures designed to foster an inclusive, safe, and harassment-free environment across all Foundation activities [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588]. The policy mandates professional conduct across all foundation interactions regardless of personal characteristics, identity, or level of experience [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588].

# Architecture / Specification

## Scope and Applicability
The Code of Conduct governs participation across [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588]:
- Governing Board meetings
- Technical Committee ([`../governance/technical-committee.md`](../governance/technical-committee.md)) meetings
- Working Group meetings (such as [`../working-groups/security-and-privacy.md`](../working-groups/security-and-privacy.md))
- Outreach Committee meetings
- Any other committee meetings and general foundation activities not otherwise governed by an independent technical project code of conduct

### AAIF Event Operations
In-person and virtual events produced by the AAIF with professional events staff are governed by the Linux Foundation Events Code of Conduct in conjunction with this foundation policy [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588].

### Technical Project Code of Conduct Boundaries
Technical projects hosted under the AAIF umbrella are organized as separate projects, and each project maintains its own code of conduct applying to participants in those specific codebases [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588].

## Expected and Prohibited Conduct
Community participants are expected to use welcoming and inclusive language, respect differing viewpoints, accept constructive criticism gracefully, and prioritize outcomes that benefit the community [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588]. Prohibited behaviors include verbal, physical, or visual harassment; discrimination based on protected characteristics; sexual attention or sexualized imagery; intimidation and stalking; sustained disruption of talks or discussions; personal attacks; and publishing private information without consent [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588].

# Lifecycle History
The foundation Code of Conduct was formally ratified and committed to the `aaif/foundation` repository as the foundational community standard governing foundation bodies and communications [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588].
