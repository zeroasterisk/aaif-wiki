# Overview
The Agentic AI Foundation Code of Conduct defines community standards, expected professional behaviors, and enforcement mechanisms across all foundation communication channels, working groups, technical repositories, and affiliated events.[^evt-foundation-pr-1]

All contributors, maintainers, and community delegates are required to adhere to these standards to maintain an inclusive, collaborative, and harassment-free environment.[^evt-foundation-pr-1]

# Architecture / Specification
## Standards and Enforcement
The policy defines unacceptable harassment and discriminatory behaviors across physical and digital spaces managed under the Linux Foundation umbrella. Violations are reported to foundation leadership and evaluated through standardized Linux Foundation community enforcement processes.[^evt-foundation-pr-1]

## Repository Compliance
The policy is anchored across AAIF repositories under the standard community health file path `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` to support automated health detection by source code platforms and CI tooling.[^evt-foundation-pr-1]

# Lifecycle History
In PR#1 of `aaif/foundation`, the repository filename was standardized from `Code_of_Conduct.md` to `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` to enable automated recognition by GitHub's community standards and repository health checklist.[^evt-foundation-pr-1]
