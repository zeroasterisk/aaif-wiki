# Overview
The Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) Code of Conduct establishes community standards, behavioral expectations, and violation handling procedures to foster an inclusive and harassment-free environment across all Foundation activities[^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588]. It mandates respectful and collaborative engagement across technical projects, working groups, committees, events, and online communication platforms[^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588].

# Operational Procedures
## Scope and Applicability
The policy formally governs all proceedings of the Governing Board, [Technical Committee](../governance/technical-committee.md), Working Groups, Outreach Committee, and any other administrative body of the AAIF, as well as general foundation activities not covered by a separate project-specific policy[^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588]. In-person and virtual events managed by professional events staff operate under the Linux Foundation Events Code of Conduct in conjunction with this foundation policy, while individual technical projects hosted under the AAIF umbrella maintain and enforce their own project-level codes of conduct[^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588].

## Expected and Unacceptable Behaviors
Community members are required to employ welcoming, inclusive language, respect differing viewpoints, accept constructive feedback gracefully, and act in the collective interest of the community[^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588]. Prohibited conduct includes verbal, visual, or physical harassment; discrimination based on protected characteristics; deliberate intimidation or stalking; sustained disruption of discussions or presentations; publication of private information without consent; and trolling or personal attacks[^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588].

## Enforcement and Appeals
Suspected violations are reported through designated foundation reporting channels and reviewed by designated community leadership[^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588]. Enforcement determinations adhere to tiered corrective consequences ranging from private reprimands to permanent expulsion from AAIF communication channels, committees, and events[^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588]. Respondents retain access to an appeals process to contest disciplinary actions[^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588].
