# Overview
The AAIF Blog Guidelines establish editorial standards, voice baselines, vendor-neutrality rules, and submission procedures for articles published across official Agentic AI Foundation channels [^evt-foundation-pr-7].

# Architecture / Specification
All submitted content and automated editorial checks must adhere to the following standards [^evt-foundation-pr-7][^evt-public-agents-pr-4]:
- **Style and Casing**: Use US English conventions and sentence case across all section headings and titles.
- **Evidence and Sourcing**: Base technical claims on direct primary sources, linking directly to specifications, RFCs, or repositories rather than secondary commentary.
- **Unsupported Claims**: Avoid categorical or absolute assertions regarding model capabilities, safety guarantees, or performance without cited empirical benchmarks.
- **Project Naming**: Adhere strictly to official foundation and working group project naming conventions.
- **Automated Verification**: Editorial helper skills automate checks for casing, tone, and citation compliance prior to human editorial review [^evt-public-agents-pr-4].

# Lifecycle History
- Established by the AAIF Foundation to govern community and working group editorial contributions.
- Updated to codify sentence case, US English, and strict primary-source referencing requirements [^evt-foundation-pr-7].
