# Overview

The Project Proposal Process specifies the submission workflow, review stages, and legal requirements for open-source initiatives seeking official hosting under the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF).[^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1] Hosting levels comprise Sandbox, Growth, and Impact projects, evaluated based on ecosystem alignment, adoption, and technical maturity.

# Architecture / Specification

### Application and Review Stages

1. **Proposal Submission**: Project maintainers file a standardized proposal in the `aaif/project-proposals` repository detailing project origin, architectural scope, community traction, and alignment with the AAIF mission.[^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1]
2. **Technical Committee (TC) Evaluation**: The [Technical Committee](../governance/technical-committee.md) assesses architectural fitness, overlap with existing projects, and designated project tier readiness.[^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1]
3. **Contribution Agreement Execution**: Based on contact information supplied in the proposal, a formal Contribution Agreement is issued to contributors. This agreement must be signed prior to final voting by the Governing Board.[^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1]
4. **Dual Approval Gate**: Formal foundation acceptance strictly mandates affirmative approval votes from both the Technical Committee (TC) and the Governing Board (GB), followed by Linux Foundation countersignature.[^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1] Projects may not represent themselves as donated or contributed until all gates are finalized.[^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1]
