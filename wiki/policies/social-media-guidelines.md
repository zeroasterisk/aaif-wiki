# Overview
The Social Media Governance and Guidelines document defines the operational strategy, editorial tone, and submission processes for official Agentic AI Foundation social channels operated under the Linux Foundation.[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637] The policy ensures that public foundation communication remains neutral, professional, inclusive, and strictly focused on open source AI ecosystem milestones and collaborative initiatives.

# Architecture / Specification

## Content Submission Channels
Community members and foundation participants can submit content for official social channels through two designated intake workflows:[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637]
- **Direct Reposts**: Community-shared links and milestone announcements submitted via the dedicated Discord amplification channel.
- **Organic Foundation Posts**: Structured requests for foundation-authored posts submitted through the content intake form.

## Platform Standards and Etiquette
Official channels tailor their engagement style across platforms while maintaining vendor-neutrality:[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637]
- **X**: Release announcements, short threads, and community highlights.
- **LinkedIn**: In-depth technical articles, case studies, and organizational achievements.
- **Bluesky**: Conversational community interaction, ideas, and open updates.

## Reposting and Moderation Rules
The Foundation restricts reposting to verified project releases, core contributor announcements, and standards implementations (such as MCP).[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637] Commercial advertisements, unsolicited vendor promotions, and personal blog posts without ecosystem-wide standard adoption are explicitly excluded. All interactions must adhere to the [Code of Conduct](../policies/code-of-conduct.md).
