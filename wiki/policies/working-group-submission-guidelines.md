# Overview
The Working Group Submission Guidelines establish the proposal intake, review, and approval process for forming new working groups under the Agentic AI Foundation [^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981].

# Architecture / Specification
Proposals are created through issue templates in the working group proposals repository and tracked publicly across proposal lifecycle stages [^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981].

## Application and Review Process
1. **Submission**: Applicants open a structured issue proposal and provide charter goals, scope, and leadership contacts [^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981].
2. **Triage & Review**: Proposals enter the backlog and are scheduled for evaluation during formal [Technical Committee](../governance/technical-committee.md) meetings [^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981].
3. **Voting**: To be accepted, a proposal must receive a simple majority vote from the Technical Committee, subject to final approval by the Governing Board [^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981].

## Status Labels
Proposals are categorized by standardized status labels [^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981]:
- `New`: The proposal is newly submitted and queued in the backlog for triage [^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981].
- `Approved`: The proposal has received required approvals [^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981].
- `Declined`: The proposal was declined with documented feedback provided in comments [^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981].
- `Waiting on Comment`: Additional clarification or revisions are required from submitters [^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981].
- `Too Early`: The proposal is premature, with guidance provided for future reapplication [^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981].
