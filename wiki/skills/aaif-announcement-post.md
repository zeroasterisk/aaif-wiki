# Overview

The `aaif-announcement-post` skill generates concise, structured LinkedIn announcement posts when RSVPs open for AAIF chapter and online events [^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-f5700710]. It extracts structured metadata from an event's `Event Tracker.docx` via `fetch_tracker.py` and produces publisher-ready copy conforming to AAIF editorial standards [^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-f5700710].

# Architecture / Specification

## Copy Structure and Editorial Voice
Posts generated by the skill follow explicit layout and stylistic constraints [^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-f5700710]:
- **Length and Layout**: Approximately 120 words structured into a one-line hook, the what/when/where particulars, speaker details with one concrete takeaway, and an RSVP call to action [^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-f5700710].
- **Formatting Limits**: Maximum of one emoji and at most three hashtags [^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-f5700710].
- **House Voice**: Emphasizes a builder-to-builder tone prioritizing technical signal and shared practice over promotional product copy [^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-f5700710].
- **Standard Standing Footer**: Concludes with standing links to the Linux Foundation Code of Conduct and Privacy Policy [^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-f5700710].

## Public-Copy Privacy Rule
To prevent leaking sensitive operational data into public channels or agent interaction traces, the skill enforces strict information withholding [^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-f5700710]:
- Suppresses private contact details including attendee intake data, speaker emails, phone numbers, and venue door codes [^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-f5700710].
- Forbids echoing withheld values back in agent confirmation dialogues, omission explanations, or transcript transcripts [^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-f5700710].
