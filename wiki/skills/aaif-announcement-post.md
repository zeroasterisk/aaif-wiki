# Overview
`aaif-announcement-post` is an operational copywriting skill that drafts structured LinkedIn event launch announcements for Agentic AI Foundation chapters and working groups.[^evt-community-events-file-a7c09511caf9-8abab7d5] It balances promotional engagement with rigorous privacy protections, speaker disclosure guidelines, and institutional compliance requirements.[^evt-community-events-file-a7c09511caf9-8abab7d5]

The skill standardizes attendee communication across global chapters to ensure that all public invitations adhere to Linux Foundation governance baselines.[^evt-community-events-file-a7c09511caf9-8abab7d5]

# Architecture / Specification
Outbound post generation adheres to strict formatting templates and content constraints evaluated by regression testing in the [Community Events Evaluation Suite](../resources/community-events-eval-suite.md):[^evt-community-events-file-a7c09511caf9-8abab7d5]

## Legal Footer Mandate
All attendee-facing copy generated by the skill must carry both standing AAIF institutional governance links in the footer:[^evt-community-events-file-a7c09511caf9-8abab7d5]
- Link to the [Code of Conduct](../policies/code-of-conduct.md).[^evt-community-events-file-a7c09511caf9-8abab7d5]
- Link to the standing Privacy Policy.[^evt-community-events-file-a7c09511caf9-8abab7d5]

Dropping either link constitutes footer drift and fails automated verification checks, as both compliance references are required for public event registrations.[^evt-community-events-file-a7c09511caf9-8abab7d5]

# Lifecycle History
Standardized legal footer requirements and regression graders were integrated into the community events skill suite to prevent regulatory and institutional policy drift across chapter event announcements.[^evt-community-events-file-a7c09511caf9-8abab7d5]
