# Overview
`aaif-dayof-slides` is an agent skill that converts event records from an AAIF chapter's tracker into structured, label-driven presentation copy for the official "Day of Event" deck (`Slides.pptx`) [^evt-community-events-file-4676d39ff431-bb8c2b01]. It populates per-event slides while strictly preserving fixed brand slides, adhering to visual identity standards, and enforcing privacy rules to prevent accidental disclosure of attendee personal data [^evt-community-events-file-4676d39ff431-bb8c2b01].

# Architecture / Specification
The skill enforces several architectural and presentation invariants [^evt-community-events-file-4676d39ff431-bb8c2b01]:

- **Brand Protection and Immutability**: Fixed slides covering AAIF global network statistics and mission overviews are marked immutable and must be preserved verbatim [^evt-community-events-file-4676d39ff431-bb8c2b01].
- **Lockup and Footer Governance**: On cover and welcome slides, the `HOSTED BY` slot holds the immutable AAIF lockup [^evt-community-events-file-4676d39ff431-bb8c2b01]. Additional logo placeholders (`LOGO 1`, `LOGO 2`) are reserved exclusively for image replacement by chapter organizers rather than textual substitution [^evt-community-events-file-4676d39ff431-bb8c2b01]. Venue hosts and participating foundation members are acknowledged explicitly in prose on slide 12 [^evt-community-events-file-4676d39ff431-bb8c2b01].
- **Public-Copy Privacy Rule**: All outputs generated by the skill are treated as public materials. Private data—including email addresses, phone numbers, venue door codes, and non-public attendee names—are strictly barred from slide copy, terminal transcripts, and conversational replies [^evt-community-events-file-4676d39ff431-bb8c2b01]. Omissions must be referenced generically rather than echoing sensitive values back into the conversation context [^evt-community-events-file-4676d39ff431-bb8c2b01].
- **Drive & File Workflow**: Drive interactions are executed using `gws` and Python, utilizing byte-level OOXML edits or Google Slides API conversion and PDF export while barring desktop rendering suites such as LibreOffice to prevent typography degradation [^evt-community-events-file-4676d39ff431-bb8c2b01].
