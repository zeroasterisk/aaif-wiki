# Overview
Bounded Autonomous Remediation is an AAIF reference architecture that resolves scoped operational problems iteratively within isolated environments governed by deterministic verification gates[^evt-wg-workflows-and-process-integration-pr-43].

# Architecture / Specification
Workflows implementing this architecture validate candidate agent remediation attempts using [deterministic acceptance gates](../patterns/deterministic-acceptance-gate.md). The minimum evidence required to clear a gate scales according to two properties of the permitted effect[^evt-wg-workflows-and-process-integration-pr-43]:
- **Blast radius**: The operational surface, dependent systems, and resource footprint affected by executing the change.
- **Reversal cost**: The complexity, downtime, and operational overhead required to back out or undo the applied effect.

Regardless of gate strength, three baseline properties must hold for every acceptance record generated by the workflow[^evt-wg-workflows-and-process-integration-pr-43]:
1. **Determinism of the verdict**: The evaluation criteria yield reproducible verdicts on identical candidate artifacts.
2. **Binding of verdict to candidate**: The acceptance verdict cryptographically or structurally binds to the exact candidate artifact evaluated.
3. **Preservation as execution evidence**: The decision record is stored intact to serve as auditable execution evidence before effect deployment.
