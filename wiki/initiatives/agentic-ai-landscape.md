# Overview

The Agentic AI Landscape initiative maintains a comprehensive, vendor-neutral inventory of open-source projects, protocol specifications, frameworks, and runtime environments across the agentic artificial intelligence ecosystem [^evt-wg-observability-and-traceability-pr-31]. It provides architectural classifications and tracks governance transitions across the Linux Foundation and AAIF landscape [^evt-wg-observability-and-traceability-pr-31].

# Architecture / Specification

### Hosted Interoperability & Mediation Projects

The landscape tracks foundational infrastructure projects hosted under AAIF [^evt-wg-observability-and-traceability-pr-31]:
- **A2A (Agent-to-Agent)**: Joined AAIF in August 2026 as the open agent-to-agent interoperability protocol layer, defining signed agent cards and cross-agent communication protocols [^evt-wg-observability-and-traceability-pr-31].
- **agentgateway**: Contributed by solo.io in June 2026, providing traffic mediation, policy enforcement, and telemetry inspection for Model Context Protocol (MCP) and A2A communications across major cloud environments [^evt-wg-observability-and-traceability-pr-31].

### Observability and Evidence Surface

These additions interface directly with the [Observability and Traceability Working Group](../working-groups/observability-and-traceability.md) by serving as policy enforcement and telemetry capture points, while highlighting ongoing research areas including the verification of signed agent card claims against runtime execution behavior [^evt-wg-observability-and-traceability-pr-31].
