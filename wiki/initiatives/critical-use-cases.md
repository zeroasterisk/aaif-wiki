# Overview
The Critical Use Cases initiative is a workstream under the Workflows and Process Integration Working Group tasked with identifying reusable workflow patterns from real, deployed agentic applications [^evt-wg-workflows-and-process-integration-pr-46]. Rather than conducting a prioritization exercise, the workstream serves as an empirical sourcing and classification initiative to catalogue production agent implementations [^evt-wg-workflows-and-process-integration-pr-46].

# Architecture / Specification
The initiative maintains standardized schemas and tracking inventories (`schema-agentic-use-cases.md` and `use-case-inventory.md`) governed by rigorous verification criteria [^evt-wg-workflows-and-process-integration-pr-46]:
- **Evidentiary Standard**: Every entry in the inventory's notes must reference a primary vendor product announcement or product documentation confirming active production deployment [^evt-wg-workflows-and-process-integration-pr-46].
- **Verification Thresholds**: Mandates at least one confirmed primary citation per use case, establishing a target threshold of three independent citations [^evt-wg-workflows-and-process-integration-pr-46].
- **Source Tiers**: Classifies source material across explicit preferred, acceptable, and non-acceptable evidence tiers to maintain attribution integrity [^evt-wg-workflows-and-process-integration-pr-46].

# Lifecycle History
The category groupings, core schema, and evidentiary standard were prepared for Working Group consensus voting, with row-level citation verification continuing under an open contribution workflow [^evt-wg-workflows-and-process-integration-pr-46].
