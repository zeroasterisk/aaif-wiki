# Overview
An **agent-initiated transaction** is an [Agentic Commerce](../working-groups/agentic-commerce.md) taxonomy classification defining a transaction triggered, negotiated, and completed autonomously by an AI agent without synchronous human intervention at execution time.[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-72]

# Architecture / Specification
Agent-initiated transactions operate within pre-established spending limits, policy constraints, and cryptographic delegation structures. They rely on standardized commerce primitives such as [agentic-checkout](./agentic-checkout.md) and [delegated-payment](./delegated-payment.md) to safely process commercial commitments across protocol boundaries.[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-72]
