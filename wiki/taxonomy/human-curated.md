# Overview
Human curated is an AAIF taxonomy term designating artifacts, catalog entries, or landscape datasets that have undergone explicit manual review and verification by human domain experts [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-39]. Maintained by the [Taxonomy and Landscape Workstream](../working-groups/taxonomy-and-landscape.md), the designation serves as an assurance signal distinguishing human-reviewed items from unverified or automated outputs [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-39].

# Architecture / Specification
The definition and scope note specify criteria for quality assurance within AAIF catalogs [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-39]:
- **Assurance Signal**: Confirms that entries reflect human validation of claims, capabilities, and classifications rather than unverified third-party scraping or automated generation [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-39].
- **Taxonomy Integration**: Included in the taxonomy schema to standardize metadata attributes across AAIF landscape projects [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-39].
