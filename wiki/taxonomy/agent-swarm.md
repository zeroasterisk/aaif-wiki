# Overview
An agent swarm is a group of [subagents](../taxonomy/subagent.md) working cooperatively or independently toward a task or shared objective[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-80].

# Architecture / Specification
Within the unified Agentic AI Foundation taxonomy schema, agent swarms are defined by several structural properties[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-80]:

- **Composition**: Comprises a collection of subagents executing parallel or collaborative subtasks.
- **Scale and Lifetime**: The specific swarm size and whether its lifetime is transient (ephemeral execution) or persistent are unspecified by default.
- **Management**: Swarms are typically scheduled, coordinated, and governed by an orchestrator agent.
