# Overview
Agentic checkout is a standardized taxonomy term established in the Agentic AI Foundation taxonomy landscape defining the workflow wherein an AI agent executes or assists the checkout phase of a commercial transaction [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-73]. It sits within the Agentic Commerce taxonomy curated in collaboration with `../working-groups/agentic-commerce.md` and `../working-groups/taxonomy-and-landscape.md` [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-73].

# Specification
Agentic checkout categorizes the interaction boundaries, state handoffs, and verification steps necessary for an AI agent to complete a transaction on behalf of a user or system [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-73]. Within the Agentic Commerce conceptual hierarchy, it coordinates with related transactional primitives, including `../taxonomy/delegated-payment.md` [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-73].
