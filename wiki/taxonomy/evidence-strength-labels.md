# Overview

Evidence-Strength Labels are a vendor-neutral appraisal vocabulary developed within the [Identity and Trust Working Group](../working-groups/identity-and-trust.md) to characterize how independently claims regarding agent identity, provenance, delegation, and consent have been verified.[^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-issue-5] The taxonomy replaces binary present/absent checks with a multi-dimensional grading model generated by a relying party verifier during appraisal, distinguishing self-asserted statements from independently corroborated and cryptographically anchored claims.[^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-issue-5]

Under this model, evidence labels are an appraisal finding produced by the verifier reflecting the actual verification depth executed, rather than self-graded assertions embedded directly within credentials.[^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-issue-5]

# Architecture / Specification

The vocabulary decouples appraisal into two distinct structural axes and an orthogonal freshness dimension:[^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-issue-5]

### Axis A: Assertion Origin (Who Asserts)
- **Declared**: The agent or its operator asserts the claim directly without independent validation.
- **Platform-attested**: The claim is vouched for by the underlying runtime, hosting platform, or execution service (e.g., [attested isolated runtimes](../patterns/attested-isolated-runtime.md)).
- **Corroborated**: An external, independent entity (such as an identity provider, merchant, or counterparty) independently validates the claim.

### Axis B: Chain Structure (How the Chain is Built)
- **Composed**: Multiple discrete attestations are bound together into a single verifiable claim (e.g., identity coupled with delegation hops and human consent). Chains are subject to the weakest-hop rule, reflecting the lowest verified hop in the sequence.
- **Anchored**: The verification chain terminates in an immutable root of trust directly verifiable by the relying party (such as a DID document, signed mandate, or hardware/CA root).

### Freshness and Revocation Dimension
Freshness, revocation status, and credential expiry are evaluated as an orthogonal dimension separate from chain depth. Verifiers evaluate real-time revocation stores and fail closed when status information is unreachable, rather than rounding up appraisal scores.[^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-issue-5]
