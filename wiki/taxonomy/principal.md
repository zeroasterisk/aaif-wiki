# Overview

Principal is a universal taxonomy term defined by the [Taxonomy and Landscape Working Group](../working-groups/taxonomy-and-landscape.md) designating the legal, organizational, or human entity on whose authority and behalf an artificial agent executes actions.[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-94]

# Scope and Conventions

Under the AAIF taxonomy, every agentic action is associated with at least one principal and may bind to multiple principals across delegated workflows.[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-94] The working group explicitly discourages ambiguous or contradictory terminology such as "principal agent" in favor of distinct designations for the authorizing entity (the principal) and the executing software entity (the agent).[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-94]
