# Overview
Delegated payment is a standardized taxonomy term within the Agentic AI Foundation taxonomy landscape designating the process by which an AI agent authorizes or executes a financial transaction under delegated authority [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-76]. The concept is established under the Agentic Commerce taxonomy domain maintained by `../working-groups/agentic-commerce.md` and `../working-groups/taxonomy-and-landscape.md` [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-76].

# Specification
Delegated payment establishes the conceptual boundaries for financial authority delegated to autonomous agents [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-76]. It defines how delegated limits, identity assertions, and payment credentials operate during transaction sequences such as `../taxonomy/agentic-checkout.md` [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-76].
