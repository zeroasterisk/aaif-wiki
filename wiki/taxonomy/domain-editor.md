# Overview
A Domain Editor is a nominated technical expert representing an active AAIF Technical Working Group who acts as a core curator authoring and evaluating shared taxonomy entries[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-eca12c0a30e2-333c16a8].

# Architecture / Specification
Domain Editors have defined governance roles in the [Taxonomy & Landscape Workstream](../working-groups/taxonomy-and-landscape.md):
- **Cross-Workgroup Review**: Advancing and approving taxonomy changes requires consensus from at least two or three Domain Editors representing different Technical Working Groups[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-eca12c0a30e2-333c16a8].
- **Anti-Erasure Guardrails**: Domain Editors hold protective sign-off authority over their domain's vocabulary; terms cannot be deleted or converted into aliases without explicit written approval from the owning group's Domain Editor[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-a54ff182c7e8-35ff909a] [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-eca12c0a30e2-333c16a8].
