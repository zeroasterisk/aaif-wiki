# Overview
An **agent-in-the-loop transaction** is an [Agentic Commerce](../working-groups/agentic-commerce.md) taxonomy classification defining a commercial transaction where an AI agent participates, assists, or mediates operational checkout steps while maintaining active human review or explicit authorization in the loop.[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-71]

The definition was submitted by the [Taxonomy and Landscape Workstream](../working-groups/taxonomy-and-landscape.md) to formalize commerce terminology and rename the earlier concept of an "agent-mediated transaction" based on working group feedback.[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-71]

# Architecture / Specification
In contrast to fully autonomous purchasing, an agent-in-the-loop transaction enforces operational boundaries and decision checkpoints where human approval is required prior to binding financial commitments (see [agentic-checkout](./agentic-checkout.md) and [delegated-payment](./delegated-payment.md)).[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-71]
