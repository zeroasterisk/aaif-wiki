# Overview

Identity is a universal taxonomy term defined within the [Taxonomy and Landscape](../working-groups/taxonomy-and-landscape.md) and [Identity and Trust](../working-groups/identity-and-trust.md) workstreams as a set of attributes related to an entity.[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-97] The definition reproduces normative phrasing from ISO/IEC 24760-1 across its 2011, 2019, and 2025 editions to align foundation terminology with international identity management standards.[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-97]

# Scope and Disambiguation

- **Non-Uniqueness**: An entity may possess multiple distinct identities depending on operational context, role, or administrative domain.[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-97]
- **Identity versus Identifier**: An identity represents the attribute set describing an entity, whereas an identifier is a specific attribute or token used to uniquely reference or address that entity within a particular scope.[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-97]
