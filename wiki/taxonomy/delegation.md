# Overview
Delegation is the process and mechanism of transferring authority, execution scope, or credentials from a principal (such as a human user or an orchestrating system) to an agent, or between collaborating agents [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-42].

# Architecture / Specification
In agentic architectures, delegation establishes the authorization boundaries and policy envelopes under which an agent executes tasks on behalf of another entity [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-42]. Delegation is closely linked to task handoff mechanisms and [context propagation](../taxonomy/context-propagation.md) across multi-agent workflows [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-42].
