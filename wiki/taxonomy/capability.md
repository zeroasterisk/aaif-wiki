# Overview

Capability is a universal taxonomy term established by the [Taxonomy and Landscape Working Group](../working-groups/taxonomy-and-landscape.md) designating an explicit ability, operation, or functional skill declared by an agent.[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-93]

# Scope and Usage

In agentic architectures, capabilities are asserted within an agent's [identity](../taxonomy/identity.md) or descriptor record and evaluated by orchestrators and registries during [resource discovery](../standards/agentic-resource-discovery.md) to match delegated tasks to appropriate agent runtimes.[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-93]
