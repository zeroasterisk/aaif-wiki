# Overview

Within the Agentic AI Foundation taxonomy, a Model is defined as a computational system capable of reading and writing both structured and unstructured text and data representations [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-86]. In agentic architectures, models serve as core inference engines that process observations, evaluate instructions, and generate intermediate reasoning steps or action invocations.

# Vocabulary and Scope

Agent workflows commonly utilize large language models (LLMs), small language models (SLMs), or vision-language models (VLMs), while the definition remains broad enough to accommodate non-language computational approaches such as hidden Markov models [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-86]. The scope encompasses the entire realization pipeline from calibrated model weights and serialization formats (such as GGUF artifacts) to input processing and execution across inference runtimes like llama.cpp [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-86]. Models interface with external capabilities through standardized mechanisms such as [tools](tool.md).
