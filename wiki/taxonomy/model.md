# Overview
A Model is a computational artifact and inference component capable of reading and generating structured and unstructured text within agentic systems [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-86].

# Architecture / Specification
In agentic AI architectures, models encompass large language models (LLMs), small language models (SLMs), and vision-language models (VLMs), as well as non-language statistical or probabilistic formulations such as hidden Markov models [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-86]. The term encompasses calibrated model weights, serialized artifacts (such as GGUF files), input/output token processing, and integration with execution runtimes (such as llama.cpp) [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-86].
