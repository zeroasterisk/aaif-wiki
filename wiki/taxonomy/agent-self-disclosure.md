# Overview

Agent self-disclosure is a universal taxonomy term defined by the [Taxonomy and Landscape Working Group](../working-groups/taxonomy-and-landscape.md) specifying the mechanism or act whereby an artificial agent explicitly identifies its automated, non-human nature to external counterparties.[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-95]

# Scope and Application

Self-disclosure applies whenever an agent engages in external interactions, such as completing an online form or negotiating with third-party web services, establishing transparency regarding artificial agency and operational origin.[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-95]
