# Overview

This policy outlines the framework for collaboration between AAIF Working Groups (WGs) and external standards bodies, industry consortia, and open source foundations (e.g., OWASP, AARM, MCP ACS) [^evt-wg-security-and-privacy-file-a17a580f85c2-2e81780d].

The goal of defining this policy is to ensure consistency in messaging, maximize influence, and consolidate efforts across the agentic AI ecosystem.

# Specification

The Technical Committee (TC) is responsible for providing guidance on the scope and posture of these engagements, particularly when WGs request clarification on how to proceed with external collaboration opportunities [^evt-wg-security-and-privacy-file-a17a580f85c2-2e81780d].

Working Group monthly reports include a section for **Ecosystem Highlights** to track relevant activity, publications, or decisions happening in external groups [^evt-wg-security-and-privacy-file-33d3ff4f1e08-f33f7a28].
