# Overview

The Agentic AI Foundation publishes case studies showcasing member organization implementations of open agentic standards such as Model Context Protocol (MCP), goose, and AGENTS.md to resolve production challenges.[^evt-foundation-file-d803a4dfa3ec-79586e70]

# Architecture / Specification

Case studies are accepted in two primary formats:
- **Written Case Study**: Open to member organizations highlighting direct or customer implementations that deliver verifiable business impact.[^evt-foundation-file-d803a4dfa3ec-79586e70]
- **Webinar Case Study**: Recorded 20–30 minute presentations with Q&A distributed across AAIF media channels.[^evt-foundation-file-d803a4dfa3ec-79586e70]

Submissions are prioritized based on agentic maturity (advancing beyond simple chatbots to autonomous systems), cross-framework interoperability, and quantitative proof of acceleration or error reduction.[^evt-foundation-file-d803a4dfa3ec-79586e70]
