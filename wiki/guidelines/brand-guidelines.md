# Overview
The Agentic AI Foundation Brand Guidelines establish official visual identity, typographic standards, design tokens, and digital asset governance for AAIF community activities, event collateral, and documentation[^evt-community-events-pr-41]. These guidelines provide deterministic rules for both human designers and automated creative agents generating event banners and presentations.

# Asset Governance and Rules
Official vector brand assets are maintained in canonical asset repositories and governed by strict integrity rules[^evt-community-events-pr-41]:
- **Canonical Asset Inventory**: Official logo vectors are cataloged in source-of-truth tables mapping filenames directly to their intended functional role.
- **Prohibition on Redrawing**: Neither human contributors nor automated generative agents may redraw or trace the AAIF mark from screenshots or raster renderings.
- **Lockup Asset Naming**: The primary lockup vector `aaif-mark.svg` provides the complete Foundation lockup despite its abbreviated filename, serving as the default mark for public event and presentation headers.
