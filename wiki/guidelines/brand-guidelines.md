# Overview
The AAIF Brand Guidelines govern visual identity, typography tokens, surface palettes, and official logo assets across Agentic AI Foundation digital repositories, event collateral, and publications [^evt-community-events-pr-41]. The guidelines ensure consistent, high-fidelity visual presentation across both human contributor assets and automated agent tooling [^evt-community-events-pr-41].

# Logo Asset Inventory and Usage Rules
Official vector assets are maintained in repository asset trees and indexed in source-of-truth tables to facilitate immediate discovery [^evt-community-events-pr-41]:

- **Authoritative Vector Assets**: Primary logo files are located in `assets/`, including `aaif-mark.svg` (which constitutes the complete full lockup despite its filename), alongside auxiliary lockups and chapter icons [^evt-community-events-pr-41].
- **Redrawing Prohibition**: Contributors and automated asset-generation agents are strictly prohibited from redrawing, tracing, or approximating the AAIF mark from screenshots or raster artifacts; all production assets must reference official vector files directly [^evt-community-events-pr-41].
- **Agent Discovery Layout**: Design documentation must surface logo inventory and asset paths in top-level tables rather than concluding sections, ensuring single-hop resolution by automated agents [^evt-community-events-pr-41].
