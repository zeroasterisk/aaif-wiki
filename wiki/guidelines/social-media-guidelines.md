# Overview
The Social Media Governance and Guidelines define the Linux Foundation operational policies, editorial voice, content submission pathways, and brand compliance rules for official Agentic AI Foundation social media presences[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637]. Foundation staff maintains final editorial and publishing control to protect consistency, accuracy, and project alignment across all official channels[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637].

# Architecture / Specification
Official social communication is designed for a global AI engineering and open-source audience, adhering to a neutral, professional, and inclusive tone across three primary channels[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637]:
- **X**: Technical announcements, release notes, punchy updates, and threads[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637].
- **LinkedIn**: Professional business discussions, member achievements, case studies, and strategic milestones[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637].
- **Bluesky**: Conversational technical dialogue and community engagement[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637].

### Content Intake and Reposting Policies
Community and member contributions are routed via two dedicated intake pathways[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637]:
1. **Direct Reposts**: Submitted through the designated AAIF Discord Social Amplification Channel for official releases, working group announcements, and Foundation-led events[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637].
2. **Organic Content**: Submitted via the structured Foundation Content Intake Form to undergo editorial review[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637].

Promotional material, vendor product advertisements, personal code tips, consulting promotions, and unsolicited commercial campaigns are explicitly barred from Foundation reposting and publishing[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637]. Community open-source projects are amplified only when directly demonstrating implementation of AAIF standards such as Model Context Protocol or Goose[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637].
