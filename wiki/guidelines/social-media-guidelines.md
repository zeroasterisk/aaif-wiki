# Overview

The Linux Foundation leads the Agentic AI Foundation social media content strategy, access, and compliance. Content is published and approved by Foundation staff to ensure consistency, accuracy, and alignment with project goals. The platforms are used to share Foundation-wide updates, including releases, milestones, events, and initiatives [^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637].

## Tone and Content

The required tone is neutral, professional, inclusive, and clear, built to serve a global AI and developer audience.

**What is posted:**
*   Official releases and large milestones.
*   Foundation-led events, initiatives, and community calls.
*   Ecosystem tools or content featured in official project work [^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637].

## Usage Guidelines

### Social Posts Baseline
Content must be relevant to agentic AI, inclusive, and avoid unsolicited promotions. Proper attribution must be given when sharing code or ideas. Platform-specific etiquette is maintained for X (short, engaging), LinkedIn (professional tone), and Bluesky (casual and conversational) [^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637].

### Reposting Policy
The official AAIF channels will repost content that is:
*   Tied to official project releases.
*   Shared by core contributors or working groups to announce project changes.
*   Connected to official Foundation events or initiatives.

The Foundation will *not* repost personal blogs, code tips, videos, vendor products, or product announcements. Community projects are featured only when they explicitly showcase the implementation of AAIF standards (e.g., MCP, goose) [^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637].
