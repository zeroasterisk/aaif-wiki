# Overview
The Social Media Governance and Guidelines define content standards, channel management, and contribution mechanisms administered by Linux Foundation staff for AAIF channels [^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637].

# Architecture / Specification
## Voice and Tone
Official social communication maintains a neutral, inclusive, professional, and vendor-neutral voice [^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637]:
- **X**: Release notes, event announcements, and short technical updates.
- **LinkedIn**: Technical case studies, milestone summaries, and business discussions.
- **Bluesky**: Conversational ecosystem interactions and community dialogue.

## Content Intake and Reposting Rules
Content is accepted and evaluated through two specific submission pathways [^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637]:
- **Direct Reposts**: Submitted via the AAIF Discord Social Amplification channel for official releases, working group updates, and community projects implementing AAIF standards.
- **Organic Foundation Posts**: Submitted via the structured Content Intake Form for broader programmatic campaigns.

Generic commercial promotions, vendor advertisements, and personal blogs are not permitted for official Foundation reposting [^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637].
