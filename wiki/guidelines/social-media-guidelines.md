# Overview
The Social Media Guidelines govern public communications, promotional copy, editorial voice, and regulatory compliance across official Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) channels and community announcements[^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-005d77f9]. These standards guide contributors, organizers, and automated agents drafting public content for platforms such as LinkedIn[^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-005d77f9].

Public communications emphasize technical substance over hype, prioritizing practitioner-focused insights while strictly guarding private participant information[^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-005d77f9].

# Architecture / Specification

## House Editorial Voice
Communications across AAIF channels must follow core tone and messaging principles[^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-005d77f9]:
- **Share the Practice, Never Sell the Product**: Copy must center on real-world engineering practices, architectural patterns, and practical trade-offs rather than vendor promotion or product sales pitches[^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-005d77f9].
- **Builder-to-Builder Authenticity**: Messaging should remain specific, grounded, and concise rather than grand or sensationalist, prioritizing signal and clarity over inflated attendance or metric claims[^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-005d77f9].

## Event Launch and Announcement Post Structure
When drafting event launch and RSVP announcements (e.g., on LinkedIn), copy must conform to a standardized, scannable format[^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-005d77f9]:
- **Length and Formatting Constraints**: Approximately 120 words in total length, containing at most one emoji and no more than three relevant hashtags[^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-005d77f9].
- **Core Elements**:
  1. A one-line introductory hook highlighting the technical topic[^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-005d77f9].
  2. Concrete logistics outlining the what, when, and where[^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-005d77f9].
  3. Featured speakers and session topic accompanied by a single tangible takeaway[^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-005d77f9].
  4. An explicit Call to Action (CTA) linking directly to the verified event RSVP platform (e.g., Luma or Gradual.AI)[^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-005d77f9].

## Public-Copy Privacy and Boundary Enforcement
All automated drafting workflows and manual publications must strictly enforce privacy protections[^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-005d77f9]:
- **PII Exclusion**: Public posts must never include personal email addresses, private phone numbers, physical door access codes, or attendee names that are not already publicly announced[^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-005d77f9]. When a non-public operational detail must be communicated, copy must describe the retrieval mechanism (e.g., "door access will be sent to registered attendees") rather than publishing the raw credential[^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-005d77f9].
- **Transcript Cleanliness**: In automated agent interactions, sensitive field omissions must refer to field names rather than echoing back the redacted values, preventing leakage in session logs and public transcripts[^evt-community-events-file-133151709c55-005d77f9].
