# Overview

The Reference Architecture Decision Template provides the standardized Architecture Decision Record (ADR) format used by the [Workflows & Process Integration Working Group](../working-groups/workflows-and-process-integration.md) to record significant structural choices, trade-offs, and governance invariants across AAIF reference architectures.[^evt-wg-workflows-and-process-integration-file-3a311f3edf5a-27d0e407]

By formalizing how decisions are proposed, debated, and adopted, the template ensures that design rationale remains auditable and that follow-on consequences and rejected alternatives are preserved alongside reference implementations.

# Architecture / Specification

Decision documents conforming to this template must implement the following sequential sections:[^evt-wg-workflows-and-process-integration-file-3a311f3edf5a-27d0e407]

1. **Decision Title**: Markdown heading identifying the decision scope.
2. **Date**: Timestamp of proposal or resolution.
3. **Status**: Lifecycle status, restricted to `Proposed`, `accepted`, `rejected`, or `superseded`, accompanied by decision issue or pull request links.
4. **Context**: Articulation of the conditions prompting the decision, detailing prior architectural state, ambiguities, or technical constraints requiring resolution.
5. **Decision**: Explicit, unambiguous declaration of the chosen pattern or architectural contract.
6. **Rationale**: Technical and operational justification supporting the choice.
7. **Consequences**: Downstream impacts resulting from adoption, covering implementation requirements, necessary follow-on tasks, and documented trade-offs.
8. **Alternatives considered**: Viable alternative architectures and explicit reasoning for why they were rejected.
9. **Follow-up**: Actionable next steps, tracking issues, or evaluation milestones.
