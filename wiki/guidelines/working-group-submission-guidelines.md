# Overview
The Working Group Submission Guidelines establish the formal intake process, evaluation pipeline, and approval requirements for community members and member organizations seeking to form a new technical working group within the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF)[^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981]. Proposals are submitted through standardized issue templates and progress through public triage and review by the [Technical Committee](../governance/technical-committee.md)[^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981].

# Architecture / Specification
## Application and Intake Workflow
Proponents initiate the formation process by opening a Working Group Proposal Application issue in the `aaif/working-group-proposals` repository[^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981]. Submitted proposals are automatically registered on the public Working Group Proposals project board and placed in the triage backlog for scheduling on a forthcoming Technical Committee (TC) meeting agenda[^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981].

## Evaluation and Voting Gates
Working group proposals are reviewed during scheduled TC meetings. Formal acceptance requires:
- An affirmative vote by a simple majority of all members of the Technical Committee[^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981].
- Final ratification by the AAIF Governing Board[^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981].

## Proposal Status Taxonomy
Proposals are tracked using a discrete lifecycle label scheme on GitHub[^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981]:
- `New`: Newly submitted proposal awaiting initial triage in the backlog.
- `Waiting on Comment`: Reviewers have requested clarifying details or revisions via issue comments.
- `Approved`: The proposal has met voting requirements and been formally accepted.
- `Declined`: The proposal has been rejected by the TC with explicit rationale recorded in the issue comments.
- `Too Early`: The proposal is not yet ready for formation; guidance for future resubmission is provided.

# Lifecycle History
The proposal intake process and status taxonomy were formalized in the `aaif/working-group-proposals` intake repository to provide an open, transparent pipeline for working group formation under TC governance[^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981].
