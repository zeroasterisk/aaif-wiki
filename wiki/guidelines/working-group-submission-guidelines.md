# Overview
The Working Group Submission Guidelines define the formal process for proposing and chartering a new working group under the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF)[^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981]. Proposals are initiated through structured issue templates in the `aaif/working-group-proposals` repository and progress through a tracked review pipeline managed by the Technical Committee.

Charter approval requires affirmative consensus from the [Technical Committee](../governance/technical-committee.md) alongside final confirmation by the Governing Board under the [Working Group Lifecycle Policy](../policies/working-group-lifecycle-policy.md)[^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981].

# Intake and Review Process
Proposals advance through three primary phases:

1. **Application Submission**: Prospective leads open a formal Working Group Proposal Application using the standardized repository issue template, detailing problem statements, proposed scope, initial deliverables, and sponsor interest[^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981].
2. **Triage and Scheduling**: Submitted proposals are logged on the public Working Group Proposals Project Board and placed into a triage backlog before being assigned a slot on an upcoming TC meeting agenda[^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981].
3. **Review and Voting**: The TC reviews the submission during public meetings. Acceptance requires a simple majority vote of all Technical Committee members, followed by formal Governing Board approval[^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981].

# Status Taxonomy
Proposals are tracked across five lifecycle states[^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981]:
- `New`: Newly submitted proposal queued in the triage backlog.
- `Approved`: Proposal formally accepted by the TC and ratified by the Governing Board.
- `Declined`: Proposal rejected with documented feedback provided in issue comments.
- `Waiting on Comment`: Evaluation paused pending additional clarification or charter refinement from submitters.
- `Too Early`: The proposal lacks prerequisites or community breadth; reviewers supply guidance for reapplication.
