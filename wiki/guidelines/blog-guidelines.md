# Overview
The Blog Guidelines set editorial standards, intake requirements, and review processes for articles published on the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) blog. The guideline instructs contributors on tone, structure, factual attribution, and branding alignment to ensure objective, high-quality technical publications across the foundation's public channels.

# Architecture / Specification
Blog content submitted for publication must conform to key editorial and stylistic invariants [^evt-foundation-pr-7]:

- **Typography and Casing**: Headers and section titles follow standard sentence case conventions.
- **Language and Spelling**: Content must be written in standard US English.
- **Evidentiary Claims**: Categorical claims must not be unsupported; technical assertions require empirical justification or explicit boundary caveats.
- **Attribution and Primary Sources**: References must link directly to authoritative primary sources rather than secondary aggregators or promotional summaries.
- **Project Naming**: Technical projects, foundation bodies, and open-source specifications must utilize official, canonical naming without marketing inflation.

Contributors authoring content are encouraged to consult ../skills/aaif-blog-guidelines.md for automated validation and workflow checks prior to submitting publication proposals.

# Lifecycle History
Maintained by foundation operations, these guidelines ensure consistent technical and editorial voice across all AAIF communication platforms. PR #7 updated the guidelines to formalize sentence case, US English spelling, strict primary-source linking, and official project naming criteria [^evt-foundation-pr-7].
