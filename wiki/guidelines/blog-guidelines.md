# Overview

The Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) blog serves as a central editorial channel on aaif.io for sharing educational, vendor-neutral technical content, release updates, and case studies across the agentic AI community [^evt-foundation-file-d56154a3412d-c180d87c]. Managed end-to-end by the Linux Foundation team, content publication operates under a standard two-week intake review lead time and requires stakeholder pre-approval before intake [^evt-foundation-file-d56154a3412d-c180d87c].

# Architecture / Specification

## Contributor Eligibility and Content Scope

Submissions are accepted from five distinct contributor categories [^evt-foundation-file-d56154a3412d-c180d87c]:

- **Member Companies:** Permitted vendor-neutral articles with a maximum of one or two closing sentences containing a single external link.
- **Project Maintainers and Contributors:** Announcements and technical deep-dives for hosted projects including Model Context Protocol (MCP), `../resources/goose.md`, and AGENTS.md.
- **Working Groups and Technical Committee:** Official updates co-authored with or endorsed by AAIF technical working groups or the `../governance/technical-committee.md`.
- **Community Contributors:** Open community technical posts, limited to two submissions per month.
- **Event Participants:** Recaps and learnings from foundation-affiliated hackathons and community programs.

## Editorial Standards and Intake Workflow

Submissions must follow narrative prose rather than marketing copy or press releases, adhere to vendor neutrality, reflect developments from the past two months, and avoid criticism of open-source projects [^evt-foundation-file-d56154a3412d-c180d87c]. The workflow proceeds through submission via the official form, intake triage, review, optional revision loops, scheduling, and publication [^evt-foundation-file-d56154a3412d-c180d87c]. Contributors can prepare and validate their submissions locally using the `../skills/aaif-blog-guidelines.md` agent skill [^evt-foundation-file-d56154a3412d-c180d87c][^evt-foundation-pr-2].
