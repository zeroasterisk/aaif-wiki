# Overview
The AAIF Blog Guidelines establish editorial standards, content scopes, and submission processes for publications on the Agentic AI Foundation official blog.

# Architecture / Specification
Guidelines define review standards and stylistic rules for contributors:
- **Tone and Voice**: Educational, neutral, and technical content focusing on open standards and agentic AI advancements.
- **Editorial Conventions**: Content must adhere to sentence case for headings and titles, standard US English spelling, and official project naming conventions [^evt-foundation-pr-7].
- **Factual Substantiation and Sourcing**: Requires primary-source linking for technical assertions and restricts unsupported categorical claims [^evt-foundation-pr-7].
- **Eligibility**: Open to working group participants, maintainers, and community members writing on Foundation projects, architectures, and research.
