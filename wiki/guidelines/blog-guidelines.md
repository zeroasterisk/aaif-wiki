# Overview
The AAIF Blog Guidelines establish editorial standards, neutrality principles, stylistic requirements, and review processes for publications on the Agentic AI Foundation blog.[^evt-foundation-pr-7] The guidelines ensure technical rigor, ecosystem vendor-neutrality, and consistent terminology across contributor submissions.

# Architecture / Specification
Editorial contributions must adhere to core stylistic and structural requirements:

- **Language and Casing**: Submissions must follow US English spelling conventions and sentence-case capitalization across titles and section headers.[^evt-foundation-pr-7]
- **Factual Claims and Neutrality**: Categorical or sweeping assertions must be backed by verifiable data; unsupported claims are prohibited.[^evt-foundation-pr-7]
- **Source Attribution**: Claims regarding external architectures, benchmarks, or protocols must provide direct primary-source hyperlinks rather than secondary aggregations.[^evt-foundation-pr-7]
- **Official Naming**: Open-source projects, working group outputs, and foundation bodies must use their official recognized names and abbreviations consistently.[^evt-foundation-pr-7]

# Lifecycle History
- **PR #7 (Open)**: Proposed standardizing sentence case, US English spelling, official project naming, and primary-source link requirements while disallowing unsupported categorical claims.[^evt-foundation-pr-7]
