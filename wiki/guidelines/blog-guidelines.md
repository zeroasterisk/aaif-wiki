# Overview
The Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) blog serves as a central hub for publishing technical insights, open source project updates, community milestones, and deployment case studies across the agentic AI ecosystem[^evt-foundation-file-d56154a3412d-c180d87c]. Publishing on aaif.io is managed end-to-end by the Linux Foundation team, with an intake process open to member companies, project maintainers, working groups, and community contributors[^evt-foundation-file-d56154a3412d-c180d87c].

# Architecture / Specification
## Contributor Eligibility and Frequency
- **Member Companies:** Content must remain vendor-neutral and mission-aligned, permitting at most two closing sentences with a single external link[^evt-foundation-file-d56154a3412d-c180d87c].
- **Project Maintainers & Contributors:** Focuses on release notes, roadmaps, and technical deep-dives for hosted projects such as Model Context Protocol (MCP), goose, and AGENTS.md[^evt-foundation-file-d56154a3412d-c180d87c].
- **Working Groups & Technical Committee:** Authored directly by working groups or the Technical Committee (`../governance/technical-committee.md`)[^evt-foundation-file-d56154a3412d-c180d87c].
- **Community Contributors:** Submissions covering tutorials and ecosystem thought leadership are capped at two posts per month per author[^evt-foundation-file-d56154a3412d-c180d87c].

## Content Requirements
Submissions require a standard two-week lead time and must satisfy specific criteria[^evt-foundation-file-d56154a3412d-c180d87c]:
- **Originality & Timeliness:** Content must be previously unpublished and reflect activity from the preceding two months[^evt-foundation-file-d56154a3412d-c180d87c].
- **Neutrality:** Strictly vendor-neutral narrative without sales pitches, promotional framing, or criticism of open source projects[^evt-foundation-file-d56154a3412d-c180d87c].
- **Format:** Narrative article format ranging between 600 and 1,500 words[^evt-public-agents-file-2aebb9feb4df-f97f79ed].
- **Automation Tooling:** Contributors can use the `aaif-blog-guidelines` skill distributed through `../project/public-agents.md` to format and check drafts[^evt-public-agents-file-2aebb9feb4df-f97f79ed][^evt-foundation-pr-2].
