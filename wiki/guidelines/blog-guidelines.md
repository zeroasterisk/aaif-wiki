# Overview
The AAIF Blog Guidelines govern submission workflows, editorial standards, and vendor neutrality requirements for all content published across Agentic AI Foundation channels [^evt-foundation-pr-7]. They ensure articles reflect community consensus, maintain technical rigor, and adhere to consistent style conventions across working groups [^evt-foundation-pr-7].

# Architecture / Specification
Submissions to the AAIF blog must satisfy the following editorial criteria [^evt-foundation-pr-7]:

- **Style and Mechanics**: Content must follow US English spelling and grammar conventions, with article titles and subheadings formatted in sentence case [^evt-foundation-pr-7].
- **Evidence and Claims**: Authors must substantiate technical statements and avoid unsupported categorical claims; references should link directly to primary sources [^evt-foundation-pr-7].
- **Nomenclature and Neutrality**: Drafts must use official project and working group names, avoiding promotional vendor framing or unverified benchmark assertions [^evt-foundation-pr-7].

# Lifecycle History
