# Overview
The Agentic AI Foundation Case Study Guidelines establish criteria and review procedures for member organizations publishing case studies on real-world adoption of open agentic AI standards [^evt-foundation-file-d803a4dfa3ec-79586e70]. Case studies highlight practical implementations of ecosystem technologies—such as MCP, goose, and AGENTS.md—focusing on agentic maturity, cross-platform interoperability, and measurable business acceleration [^evt-foundation-file-d803a4dfa3ec-79586e70].

# Submission Formats and Requirements
AAIF accepts case studies through two primary formats [^evt-foundation-file-d803a4dfa3ec-79586e70]:

- **Written Case Studies**: Long-form articles outlining business challenges, technical architectures, adoption phases, and quantified business outcomes [^evt-foundation-file-d803a4dfa3ec-79586e70].
- **Webinar Case Studies**: Recorded 20–30 minute presentations with Q&A sessions distributed across foundation channels [^evt-foundation-file-d803a4dfa3ec-79586e70].

All submissions require documented tangible impact and must maintain vendor neutrality and factual accuracy [^evt-foundation-file-d803a4dfa3ec-79586e70].
