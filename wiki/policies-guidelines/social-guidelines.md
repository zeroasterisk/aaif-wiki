# Overview
The Social Media Governance and Guidelines govern content strategy, public communication standards, and social media amplification for the Agentic AI Foundation under Linux Foundation leadership[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637]. The policy outlines platform tone, submission workflows, and criteria for featuring open-source agentic AI developments across official foundation channels.

# Architecture / Specification

## Communication Tone & Baseline
Official AAIF channels maintain a neutral, professional, inclusive, and vendor-neutral tone tailored to a global developer and artificial intelligence audience[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637]. Key baseline principles include:
- **Domain Relevance**: Focus strictly on the open-source AI ecosystem, official releases, community events, and technical resources.
- **Neutrality & Anti-Promotion**: Generic self-promotion, proprietary product marketing, consulting pitches, and sponsored content are prohibited.
- **Attribution**: Proper attribution for open-source licenses, technical contributions, and community work is required.
- **Platform-Specific Style**: Tailored engagement across platforms, including punchy updates on X, professional case studies and technical milestones on LinkedIn, and conversational dialogue on Bluesky.

## Submission and Reposting Workflow
Content intake is managed through distinct channels depending on the amplification format[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637]:
- **Direct Reposts**: Submitted via dedicated foundation Discord amplification channels for official releases, working group announcements, and foundation-led events.
- **Organic AAIF Posts**: Submitted through the foundation content intake form for structured consideration.
- **Community Projects**: Highlighted exclusively when demonstrating concrete implementation of AAIF standards and specifications.
