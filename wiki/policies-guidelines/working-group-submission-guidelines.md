# Overview
The Working Group Submission Guidelines define the formal intake, review, and voting procedure for establishing new working groups within the Agentic AI Foundation[^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981]. Proposals are submitted asynchronously via issue templates and evaluated by the [Technical Committee](../governance/technical-committee.md)[^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981].

# Intake and Review Process
Applicants submit a structured Working Group Proposal issue to the `aaif/working-group-proposals` repository[^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981]. Submissions are tracked on the Working Group Proposals Board and triaged for discussion in scheduled Technical Committee meetings[^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981].

## Status Labels
Proposals are categorized using five formal tracking states[^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981]:
- `New`: Newly submitted proposal placed in the triage backlog.
- `Approved`: Accepted by the Technical Committee and Governing Board.
- `Declined`: Proposal rejected with explanatory comments.
- `Waiting on Comment`: Clarifications or additional information requested by reviewers.
- `Too Early`: Proposal not yet mature enough for WG status, accompanied by guidance on reapplication.

## Approval Criteria
To be accepted, a working group proposal requires a simple majority vote from members of the Technical Committee and remains subject to final approval by the Governing Board[^evt-working-group-proposals-file-b33563055168-33233981].
