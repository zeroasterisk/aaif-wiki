# Overview

The AAIF Blog Guidelines establish editorial standards, contributor criteria, and style rules for content published across official Agentic AI Foundation channels.[^evt-foundation-pr-7]

# Architecture / Specification

Content submitted for publication must adhere to the following editorial standards:

- **Tone and Case Conventions**: Use sentence case for headings and titles, and maintain standard US English spelling across all publications.[^evt-foundation-pr-7]
- **Evidence and Sourcing**: All substantive technical claims must link directly to primary sources; unsupported categorical claims are strictly prohibited.[^evt-foundation-pr-7]
- **Naming Consistency**: Projects, working groups, and foundation entities must use official project naming conventions as established in the AAIF landscape and project governance registries.[^evt-foundation-pr-7]
- **Vendor Neutrality**: Content must remain neutral, avoiding product endorsements or proprietary framing while emphasizing open standards and reference implementations.
