## taxonomy

- [Action Record](taxonomy/action-record.md) — Tamper-evident, content-private, and independently verifiable record proving what an agent executed, distinct from operator telemetry and authorization grants. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Agent-In-The-Loop Transaction](taxonomy/agent-in-the-loop-transaction.md) — Agentic Commerce taxonomy term defining a commercial transaction where an AI agent mediates or executes workflow steps under human oversight. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Agent-Initiated Transaction](taxonomy/agent-initiated-transaction.md) — Agentic Commerce taxonomy term defining a commercial transaction triggered and executed autonomously by an AI agent under delegated authority. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Agent Registry](taxonomy/agent-registry.md) — Centralized or federated directory storing validated identities, metadata, capabilities, and governance policies for discoverable AI agents. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Agent Self-Disclosure](taxonomy/agent-self-disclosure.md) — Universal taxonomy term defining an agent's explicit identification of its artificial nature during interaction with external systems or users. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Agent Swarm](taxonomy/agent-swarm.md) — Universal taxonomy term defining a group of subagents working cooperatively or independently toward a shared objective. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Agent-to-agent](taxonomy/agent-to-agent.md) — Universal taxonomy term defining direct communication, task delegation, and coordination interactions between autonomous agents. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Agentic Checkout](taxonomy/agentic-checkout.md) — Agentic Commerce taxonomy term defining the agent-driven or agent-assisted workflow completing an e-commerce checkout transaction. _(draft, unverified)_
- [AI Agent Bill of Materials](taxonomy/ai-agent-bill-of-materials.md) — Machine-readable inventory defining the foundation models, harness software, tools, and third-party dependencies comprising an AI agent system. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Capability](taxonomy/capability.md) — Universal taxonomy term defining a declared ability or function of an agent asserted in an identity record and matched during discovery. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Context](taxonomy/context.md) — Universal taxonomy term defining the information assembled and available to an agent when making a decision during an execution step. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Context Exhaustion](taxonomy/context-exhaustion.md) — Universal taxonomy term defining the session failure mode where task-required information exceeds model context window capacity. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Continuous Assurance](taxonomy/continuous-assurance.md) — Ongoing automated or semi-automated verification evaluating an agent system against an approved specification throughout its operational lifecycle. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Cross-Client Data Leakage](taxonomy/cross-client-data-leakage.md) — Security vulnerability where confidential session state, prompt data, or tool responses cross isolation boundaries on a shared agent tool server. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Delegated Payment](taxonomy/delegated-payment.md) — Agentic Commerce taxonomy term defining the authorization and execution of a financial payment by an agent under delegated authority. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Emission Conformant](taxonomy/emission-conformant.md) — Taxonomy classification designating an artifact claim asserting structural schema validity at the emission boundary without operational behavioral guarantees. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Evidence-Strength Labels](taxonomy/evidence-strength-labels.md) — Two-axis appraisal vocabulary categorizing identity, delegation, and consent claims by assertion source, chain composition, and verification depth. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Guardrail](taxonomy/guardrail.md) — Universal taxonomy term defining a model-level constraint that prescribes intended actions or prevents unwanted actions and disclosures. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Hallucination](taxonomy/hallucination.md) — Universal taxonomy term defining a facially plausible detail or assertion in AI output that lacks evidential support or ground-truth basis. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Identity](taxonomy/identity.md) — Universal taxonomy term defining a set of attributes related to an entity across agentic authentication, delegation, and discovery. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Intended Purpose](taxonomy/intended-purpose.md) — The documented use, conditions, and context for which an AI system is designated by its provider prior to deployment. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Mandate](taxonomy/mandate.md) — Agentic Commerce taxonomy term defining an explicit authorization and boundary agreement governing an agent's authority to act or transact on behalf of a principal. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Model](taxonomy/model.md) — Universal taxonomy term defining a computational artifact capable of reading and generating structured and unstructured representations. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Operationally Conformant](taxonomy/operationally-conformant.md) — Taxonomy classification designating a claim asserting that runtime system or agent behavior satisfies specification requirements regardless of record schema validation. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Principal](taxonomy/principal.md) — Universal taxonomy term designating an entity on whose behalf an agent acts or executes actions. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Prompt](taxonomy/prompt.md) — Universal taxonomy term defining the explicit input instructions and contextual data provided to a model to steer its output or behavior. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Rug Pull](taxonomy/rug-pull.md) — Agent tool supply-chain attack where a previously verified tool definition, package, or endpoint is silently replaced with malicious behavior. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Self-Reported](taxonomy/self-reported.md) — Taxonomy classification denoting observation records emitted directly by an artifact about itself without independent verification outside its write path. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Skill](taxonomy/skill.md) — Universal taxonomy term defining a packaged, reusable set of instructions loaded into context on demand to guide agent task performance. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Subagent](taxonomy/subagent.md) — Universal taxonomy term defining an agent spawned by an existing agent without inherent inheritance of identity or permissions. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Three-State Reconciliation](taxonomy/three-state-reconciliation.md) — Evaluation model comparing an agent claim against independent observation resolved into agreement, contradiction, or no independent evidence. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Tool](taxonomy/tool.md) — Universal taxonomy term defining an external function, API, or service an agent may call to retrieve data or perform an action. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Tool Definition Verification](taxonomy/tool-definition-verification.md) — Process of validating the authenticity, schema integrity, and behavioral claims of an agent tool definition or manifest prior to runtime admission. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Tool Poisoning](taxonomy/tool-poisoning.md) — Supply-chain attack where an agent tool or tool definition is deliberately crafted or modified to execute malicious actions or alter agent behavior. _(draft, unverified)_
- [User](taxonomy/user.md) — Universal taxonomy term distinguishing human or client counterparties interacting with interfaces from the operators directing agent execution. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Witness Scope](taxonomy/witness-scope.md) — Taxonomy classification designating the custody category of an entity holding or attesting an agent action record. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Workflow Primitives](taxonomy/workflow-primitives.md) — Canonical building-block vocabulary (P1–P19) defining execution steps, boundary contracts, triggers, and control gates across agentic workflows. _(draft, unverified)_

## working-groups

- [Accuracy and Reliability Working Group](working-groups/accuracy-and-reliability.md) — AAIF technical working group establishing failure taxonomies, evaluation benchmarks, and engineering guidelines for dependable agent execution. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Agent Context and Memory Working Group](working-groups/agent-context-and-memory.md) — Proposed AAIF technical working group defining standards, reference architectures, and lifecycle semantics for agent context and memory systems. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Agentic Commerce Working Group](working-groups/agentic-commerce.md) — Technical working group establishing vendor-neutral capability maps, protocol gap analyses, payment standards, and commerce primitives for autonomous agents. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Attestation and Evidence Working Group](working-groups/attestation-and-evidence.md) — Cross-cutting AAIF technical working group defining vendor-neutral standards, verification taxonomies, and portable evidence bundles for independently checkable agent actions. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Governance, Risk & Regulatory Alignment Working Group](working-groups/governance-risk-and-regulatory.md) — Technical working group bridging global AI policy developments and technical agentic systems through regulation tracking, governance patterns, and audit record standards. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Identity and Trust Working Group](working-groups/identity-and-trust.md) — Technical working group defining vendor-neutral standards, delegation frameworks, and verification taxonomies for agent identity and authorization. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Observability and Traceability Working Group](working-groups/observability-and-traceability.md) — AAIF technical working group establishing vendor-neutral agent telemetry standards, OpenTelemetry semantic conventions, and cross-framework trace interoperability. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Security and Privacy Working Group](working-groups/security-and-privacy.md) — Technical working group establishing open threat models, security design patterns, and operational controls for agentic AI systems. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Taxonomy & Landscape Workstream](working-groups/taxonomy-and-landscape.md) — Cross-cutting AAIF workstream curating a shared SKOS-Lite taxonomy and ecosystem map across foundation technical working groups. _(draft, unverified)_
- [Workflows and Process Integration Working Group](working-groups/workflows-and-process-integration.md) — AAIF technical working group establishing shared workflow execution models, reference architectures, boundary patterns, and activity-based composition taxonomies. _(draft, unverified)_
