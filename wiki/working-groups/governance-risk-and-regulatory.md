# Overview

The Governance, Risk, and Regulatory Working Group (WG Government, Risk & Regulatory Alignment) aligns technical agentic AI architectures with evolving international legal frameworks, regional AI governance instruments, and corporate risk policies [^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-60f28464dd94-576a1dc7]. The group translates regulatory requirements into actionable engineering guidance, policy schemas, and technical compliance baselines for autonomous agent deployments.

The working group is co-chaired by Deborah Eng (JPMorgan Chase) and Sriram Natarajan (Google) [^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-60f28464dd94-576a1dc7].

# Workstreams and Deliverables

- **Regional Regulatory Gap Analysis (Phase 2):** Evaluates regional and international agentic AI regulatory instruments against a standardized 16-theme governance baseline [^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-60f28464dd94-576a1dc7]. Standardized prompt-driven evaluation methods ensure uniform comparative rigor across contributor jurisdictions.
- **Operationalizing Agent Governance:** A cross-cutting framework defining concrete engineering patterns for runtime agent oversight, alignment verification, and lifecycle supervision, submitted to the [Technical Committee](../governance/technical-committee.md) for consortium-wide alignment [^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-60f28464dd94-576a1dc7].
- **Legal Context Schema:** A structured schema standardizing machine-readable legal constraints, contractual boundaries, and regulatory parameters ingested by agent execution engines [^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-60f28464dd94-576a1dc7].
- **Agentic Evidence Collection Best Practice Guide:** Standardized guidelines for capturing audit-grade telemetry, authorization records, and forensic execution traces in compliance with regulatory oversight standards [^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-60f28464dd94-576a1dc7].
