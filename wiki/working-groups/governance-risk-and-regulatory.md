# Overview

The Governance, Risk, and Regulatory Working Group (also referred to as Government, Risk & Regulatory Alignment) serves as the bridge between global AI policy developments and the technical implementation of governance across agent systems [^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-fa4050a9f626-ee69c81a].

The working group is chaired by Ryan Hagemann (IBM) and Deborah Eng (JPMorgan Chase) [^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-fa4050a9f626-ee69c81a].

# Architecture / Specification

### Objectives & Methodology
- **Policy Instrument Landscape**: Analyzing global regulatory frameworks, best practices, standards, and legislative instruments applicable to agentic AI systems [^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-fa4050a9f626-ee69c81a].
- **Thematic Policy Mapping**: Mapping policy instruments against core governance themes to identify common regulatory requirements [^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-fa4050a9f626-ee69c81a].
- **Gap Analysis**: Developing structured gap analyses comparing regulatory requirements against existing technical specifications and runtime implementations [^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-fa4050a9f626-ee69c81a].
- **Information Extraction Tooling**: Designing standardized AI prompts for structured, consistent extraction and synthesis of legal and standards texts [^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-fa4050a9f626-ee69c81a].
