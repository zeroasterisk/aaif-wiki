# Overview

The Governance, Risk, and Regulatory Working Group (WG Government, Risk & Regulatory Alignment) is an AAIF working group chaired by Ryan Hagemann (IBM) and Deborah Eng (JPMorgan Chase)[^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-fa4050a9f626-ee69c81a]. The group bridges global policy developments, legislation, and industry standards with technical specifications for agentic AI systems.

# Architecture / Specification

The working group's charter deliverables follow a three-phase progression[^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-fa4050a9f626-ee69c81a]:

1. **Phase 1: Landscape Analysis**: Identifying and cataloging relevant policy instruments, legislative frameworks, and best practices across global jurisdictions.
2. **Phase 2: Thematic Mapping and Gap Analysis**: Mapping policy instruments against approximately a dozen common governance and risk themes, developing AI prompt workflows for consistent extraction, and synthesizing gap analysis findings.
3. **Phase 3: Recommendations and Final Deliverable**: Publishing technical alignment recommendations and guidance for AAIF working groups and member organizations.

# Lifecycle History

- **2026-07**: Completed the initial landscape analysis phase and established consensus on initial policy instruments and thematic categories, commencing Phase 2 gap analysis mapping[^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-fa4050a9f626-ee69c81a].
