# Overview
The Governance, Risk & Regulatory Alignment (GRRA) Working Group serves as a bridge between global AI policy developments and technical advancements in agentic AI systems within the Agentic AI Foundation [^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-d22b3d6550c3-13ea07bf]. Approved on June 1, 2026, and chaired by Deborah Eng with co-chair Sriram Natarajan, the working group addresses gaps in cohesive regulatory guidance, standards interoperability, and risk mitigation strategies for organizational agent deployments [^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-d22b3d6550c3-13ea07bf].

# Scope and Deliverables
The working group's technical activities span policy monitoring, governance architecture, and compliance gap analysis [^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-d22b3d6550c3-13ea07bf]:

- **Policy and Standards Tracking**: Identifying and monitoring emerging global AI regulations and standards impacting agentic systems, including the EU AI Act, TC260 guidelines, and national agentic frameworks [^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-d22b3d6550c3-13ea07bf, ^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-issue-9].
- **Organizational Protocol Governance**: Examining enterprise implementation of protocols such as MCP within existing corporate governance processes [^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-d22b3d6550c3-13ea07bf].
- **Future Specifications**: Developing proposals for an [AI Agent Bill of Materials](../taxonomy/ai-agent-bill-of-materials.md), action reversibility and undo infrastructure, human-in-the-loop patterns, incident reporting policies, and vertical domain frameworks like the FINOS AI Governance Framework [^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-d22b3d6550c3-13ea07bf].
- **Phase 2 Gap Analysis**: Evaluating conformity anchors for tamper-evident logging across converging standards including ISO/IEC 24970, prEN 18229-1, and IETF draft-sharif-agent-audit-trail, distinguishing self-reported telemetry from independently attestable records [^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-issue-9].

Explicitly excluded from the group's scope is providing formal legal advice or commercial compliance solutions [^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-d22b3d6550c3-13ea07bf].
