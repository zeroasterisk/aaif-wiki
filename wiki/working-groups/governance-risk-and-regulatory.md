# Overview
The Governance, Risk, and Regulatory Alignment Working Group (WG Government, Risk & Regulatory Alignment) aligns emerging international regulatory instruments, risk management taxonomies, and compliance standards with operational agent implementations.[^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-60f28464dd94-576a1dc7] The group is chaired by Deborah Eng (JPMorgan Chase) and co-chaired by Sriram Natarajan (Google).[^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-60f28464dd94-576a1dc7]

# Deliverables and Workstreams
- **Regulatory Gap Analysis:** The working group benchmarks regional and international agentic AI policies and regulatory instruments against an established 16-theme baseline.[^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-60f28464dd94-576a1dc7] In Phase 2, the group standardized prompt-driven policy evaluation methods to ensure consistent cross-jurisdictional assessments by regional contributors.[^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-60f28464dd94-576a1dc7]
- **Operationalizing Agent Governance:** A core proposal submitted to the Technical Committee (`../governance/technical-committee.md`) defining practical frameworks for implementing runtime risk controls, policy gates, and organizational oversight across agent fleets.[^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-60f28464dd94-576a1dc7]
- **Legal Context Schema:** An upcoming structural schema for expressing legal, compliance, and jurisdictional constraints in machine-readable formats for agent reasoning and execution.[^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-60f28464dd94-576a1dc7]
- **Agentic Evidence Collection Best Practice Guide:** Builder guidance specifying verifiable logging and evidence preservation techniques required for regulatory auditability and accountability.[^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-60f28464dd94-576a1dc7]
