# Overview

The Observability & Traceability Working Group establishes open telemetry protocols, line-level tracing architectures, and cross-layer debugging specifications across agentic runtime environments [^evt-wg-observability-and-traceability-pr-33].

The group coordinates standard telemetry formats spanning low-level kernel tracing, GPU runtime events, and high-level agent action attribution [^evt-wg-observability-and-traceability-pr-33].

# Architecture / Specification

### Cross-Group & External Liaisons
The working group maintains an intake tracker and liaison roster for coordination across standards bodies and external initiatives [^evt-wg-observability-and-traceability-pr-33]:
- **External Standards Bodies**: OpenTelemetry GenAI SIG, OWASP AOS, W3C Trace Context, Agent Trace, Monocle, and AGNTCY [^evt-wg-observability-and-traceability-pr-33].
- **Internal AAIF Touchpoints**: Technical Committee, sibling working groups (Security & Privacy, Workflows & Process Integration, Governance, Risk & Regulatory, Agentic Commerce), and the Taxonomy & Landscape workstream [^evt-wg-observability-and-traceability-pr-33].
- **Inbound & Outbound Tracking**: Explicit tracking of issue flows and standardization requests across related initiatives [^evt-wg-observability-and-traceability-pr-33].
