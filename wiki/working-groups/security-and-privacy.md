# Overview
The Security and Privacy Working Group (WG) advances the Agentic AI Foundation's mission by establishing security and privacy as a shared discipline across the Agentic AI ecosystem. The WG produces open threat models, a shared taxonomy, and design patterns intended for adoption by providers, consumers, and peer working groups to share responsibility for trustworthy agentic AI [^evt-wg-security-and-privacy-file-b33563055168-a40a890b].

# Deliverables
The WG is actively developing several key deliverables:
*   The Agentic AI Security Best Practices Guide, with the first version frozen and merged for internal socialization and public promotion [^evt-wg-security-and-privacy-file-5d17bf85b2af-7c215d2d].
*   A catalog of reusable design patterns, including kill-switch patterns, with memory-related patterns planned [^evt-wg-security-and-privacy-file-5d17bf85b2af-7c215d2d].
*   A code-facing deliverable converting security best practices into actionable static-analysis rules [^evt-wg-security-and-privacy-file-5d17bf85b2af-7c215d2d].
*   Contributions to the unified AAIF taxonomy, targeting an initial version release by the end of September 2026 [^evt-wg-security-and-privacy-file-5d17bf85b2af-7c215d2d].

# Leadership
The WG is chaired by Alex Frazer (Runlayer) and co-chaired by Junjie Bu (Google). Their term runs from March 1, 2026, to January 31, 2027 [^evt-wg-security-and-privacy-file-b33563055168-a40a890b].
