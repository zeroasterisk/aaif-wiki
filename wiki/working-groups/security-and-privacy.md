# Overview
The Security and Privacy Working Group (S&P WG) advances the Agentic AI Foundation's mission by establishing security and privacy as a shared discipline across the Agentic AI ecosystem [^evt-wg-security-and-privacy-file-b33563055168-a40a890b].

The group produces open threat models, a shared taxonomy, and design patterns that providers, consumers, and peer working groups can adopt to share responsibility for trustworthy agentic AI [^evt-wg-security-and-privacy-file-b33563055168-a40a890b].
