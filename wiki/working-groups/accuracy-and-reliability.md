# Overview
The Accuracy and Reliability Working Group (ARWG) develops standards, evaluation methodologies, and engineering practices to ensure agentic AI systems operate reliably, correctly, and consistently in production environments[^evt-wg-accuracy-and-reliability-file-fa4050a9f626-df96ac2e].

# Deliverables and Initiatives
- **D1 (Terminology and Methodology)**: Methodological grounding and literature review establishing terminology across outcome correctness, consistency across runs, robustness to perturbations, and operational reliability[^evt-wg-accuracy-and-reliability-file-fa4050a9f626-df96ac2e]. Contributions are aligned into the cross-group [Taxonomy & Landscape](../working-groups/taxonomy-and-landscape.md) workstream.
- **D2 (Enterprise Gap Analysis)**: Practitioner-focused analysis identifying enterprise requirements, adoption personas, and actionable gaps in current research benchmarks[^evt-wg-accuracy-and-reliability-file-fa4050a9f626-df96ac2e].
- **Cross-WG Liaisons (I1)**: Establishing coordination channels with other AAIF groups, including the [Observability & Traceability](../working-groups/observability-and-traceability.md) Working Group[^evt-wg-accuracy-and-reliability-file-fa4050a9f626-df96ac2e].
