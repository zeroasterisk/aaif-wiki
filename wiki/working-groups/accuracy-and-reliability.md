# Overview

The Accuracy and Reliability Working Group (ARWG) establishes measurement frameworks, standardized evaluation benchmarks, empirical surveys, and architectural reliability patterns across agentic AI systems.[^evt-wg-accuracy-and-reliability-pr-10]

# Architecture / Specification

The working group's scope covers:

- Developing standardized evaluation and trigger verification methodologies (such as [Agent Skill Evaluation](../guidelines/agent-skill-evaluation.md)).
- Conducting empirical baseline surveys and reviews across production agent deployments (Deliverables D1 and D2).[^evt-wg-accuracy-and-reliability-pr-10]
- Coordinating cross-working-group reliability standards and Phase 2 roadmap alignment under the [Technical Committee](../governance/technical-committee.md).[^evt-wg-accuracy-and-reliability-pr-10]

# Lifecycle History

The working group operates under the Technical Committee governance framework. ARWG tracked deliverable reviews for D1 and the D2 empirical survey in its August 2026 reporting cycle.[^evt-wg-accuracy-and-reliability-pr-10]
