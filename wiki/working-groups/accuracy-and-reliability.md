# Overview

The Accuracy and Reliability Working Group (ARWG) is an Agentic AI Foundation technical body establishing measurement frameworks, empirical evaluation methodologies, benchmark inventories, and developer surveys to assess AI agent precision, consistency, and operational safety.

# Activities and Deliverables

The working group maintains industry benchmarking initiatives and community surveys to evaluate production agent accuracy[^evt-wg-accuracy-and-reliability-pr-9].

## Platform-Neutral Reliability Survey

ARWG developed a 16-question platform-neutral survey assessing agent deployment challenges and reliability practices across five functional sections[^evt-wg-accuracy-and-reliability-pr-9]:
- **Eligibility**: Initial qualification screening for agent practitioners.
- **About the System**: Agent system architecture characteristics, operational models, and framework configurations.
- **Benefits and Challenges**: Perceived operational benefits, failure modes, and deployment obstacles.
- **Accuracy and Reliability**: Empirical reliability baselines, hallucination controls, and measurement practices.
- **Future Priorities**: Practitioner needs for standardized benchmarks, evaluation tooling, and deterministic verification.

# Cross-Working Group Collaboration

ARWG coordinates with the [Observability and Traceability Working Group](../working-groups/observability-and-traceability.md) and [Workflows and Process Integration Working Group](../working-groups/workflows-and-process-integration.md) on empirical testing of agent behavior models and acceptance gates.
