# Overview
The Accuracy and Reliability Working Group develops vendor-neutral evaluation standards, failure classification taxonomies, benchmark frameworks, recovery patterns, and quality metrics for autonomous AI agents [^evt-wg-accuracy-and-reliability-file-922f59c26329-135870e7]. The group aims to systematically reduce non-deterministic errors, hallucination risks, and execution drift in agentic workflows.

The working group is chaired by Jordan Auge (Cisco) and Casper Nielsen (Diagrid) and contributes directly to the cross-cutting [Taxonomy and Landscape Workstream](../workstreams/taxonomy-and-landscape.md) [^evt-wg-accuracy-and-reliability-file-922f59c26329-135870e7].

# Architecture / Specification
The working group's active roadmap focuses on two core deliverables [^evt-wg-accuracy-and-reliability-file-922f59c26329-135870e7]:

- **Deliverable 1 (D1 - Terminology & Taxonomy)**: Defining structured error classification, hallucination taxonomies, and recovery terminology batches for shared cross-WG alignment [^evt-wg-accuracy-and-reliability-file-922f59c26329-135870e7].
- **Deliverable 2 (D2 - Gap Analysis & Practitioner Survey)**: Gathering empirical operational pain points, error recovery challenges, and prioritization feedback from industry practitioners with firsthand agentic deployment experience [^evt-wg-accuracy-and-reliability-file-922f59c26329-135870e7].
