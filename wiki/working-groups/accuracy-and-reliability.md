# Overview
The Accuracy and Reliability Working Group (ARWG) is an AAIF technical body focused on defining metrics, benchmarking methodologies, and reference datasets to assess and improve the operational dependability of agentic systems [^evt-wg-accuracy-and-reliability-pr-10]. The group produces structured failure taxonomies and empirical practitioner surveys to quantify production agent failure modes [^evt-wg-accuracy-and-reliability-pr-10].

# Architecture / Specification
The working group organizes its technical agenda around core deliverable tracks and multi-stage evaluation frameworks [^evt-wg-accuracy-and-reliability-pr-10]:

- **Deliverable 1 (D1) Failure Taxonomy**: Synthesizes structured failure classifications, characterizing non-deterministic and cascading errors in agentic workflows [^evt-wg-accuracy-and-reliability-pr-10].
- **Deliverable 2 (D2) Practitioner Survey**: Coordinates community survey instruments, including the [accuracy and reliability survey](../resources/accuracy-and-reliability-survey.md), to gather empirical reliability data from real-world agent deployments [^evt-wg-accuracy-and-reliability-pr-10].
- **Cross-WG Coordination**: Aligns evaluation criteria with related technical initiatives, such as [Security and Privacy](../working-groups/security-and-privacy.md) and [Workflows and Process Integration](../working-groups/workflows-and-process-integration.md), in preparation for Phase 2 deliverables [^evt-wg-accuracy-and-reliability-pr-10].
