# Overview

The Accuracy and Reliability Working Group (ARWG) focuses on developing open standards, evaluation methodologies, and practical benchmarks for assessing accuracy, consistency, robustness, and operational reliability in agentic AI systems.[^evt-wg-accuracy-and-reliability-file-fa4050a9f626-df96ac2e]

# Key Deliverables and Initiatives

- **D1 (Terminology and Methodology)**: A supporting deliverable providing academic and literature grounding for agent reliability. Terminology execution is structured into three milestones; Milestone 1 covers 41 terms structured around outcome correctness, consistency across runs, perturbation robustness, and operational system reliability.[^evt-wg-accuracy-and-reliability-file-fa4050a9f626-df96ac2e] Initial terms feed into the shared [Taxonomy](../specification/taxonomy.md) effort.
- **D2 (Enterprise Gap Analysis)**: An initiative analyzing practitioner pain points, requirements, and gaps blocking enterprise adoption, transitioning from a pure benchmark inventory toward practitioner-grounded evaluation frameworks.[^evt-wg-accuracy-and-reliability-file-fa4050a9f626-df96ac2e]
- **Cross-WG Liaisons**: Active coordination with related working groups, including an initial liaison established with [Observability and Traceability](observability-and-traceability.md).[^evt-wg-accuracy-and-reliability-file-fa4050a9f626-df96ac2e]
