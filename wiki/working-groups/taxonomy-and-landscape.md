# Overview
The Taxonomy and Landscape Workstream (WS Taxonomy & Landscape) is a cross-cutting Agentic AI Foundation initiative tasked with curating a common, rigorous vocabulary across all technical working groups and maintaining an ecosystem landscape of agentic technologies.[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-440eb159591a-19b34556] The workstream is co-chaired by Junjie Bu (Google) and Gala Malbasic (Bloomberg).[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-60f28464dd94-f4687b4a]

# Operational Model and Review Governance
The workstream operates through nominated Domain Editors representing individual AAIF working groups.[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-60f28464dd94-f4687b4a] Domain Editors submit candidate terms originating in their respective working groups, draft definitions in alignment with their domains, and conduct formal peer reviews.[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-60f28464dd94-f4687b4a]

Under the merge governance approved in September 2026:
- **Asynchronous Merges:** Definition pull requests merge asynchronously once two Domain Editors approve and all active review comments are resolved.[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-60f28464dd94-f4687b4a]
- **Live Votes:** Synchronous voting sessions are reserved exclusively for contested terms or unresolved disputes.[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-60f28464dd94-f4687b4a]
- **Ready for Review Milestone:** Pull requests that have completed preliminary intake and group review are assigned to a structured milestone to insulate reviewers from unsolicited or AI-generated submissions.[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-60f28464dd94-f4687b4a]

# Deliverables and Status
- **Taxonomy:** As of September 2026, 8 core definitions have merged (including terms covering human oversight and identity fundamentals), with dozens more undergoing cross-working-group review across Observability, Workflows, Security, and Commerce domains.[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-60f28464dd94-f4687b4a]
- **Landscape:** Work on the technology landscape deliverable was formally paused in September 2026 to focus full bandwidth on resolving the core taxonomy, pending the establishment of neutral inclusion criteria approved by the Technical Committee (`../governance/technical-committee.md`).[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-60f28464dd94-f4687b4a]

# Lifecycle History
Formed in mid-2026, the workstream established initial schemas and intake pipelines in June 2026, initiated systematic backlog reviews in July and August, and established its asynchronous two-editor approval standard in September 2026.[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-440eb159591a-19b34556][^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-60f28464dd94-f4687b4a]
