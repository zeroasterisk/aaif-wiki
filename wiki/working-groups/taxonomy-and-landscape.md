# Overview

The Taxonomy and Landscape Working Group (WS Taxonomy & Landscape) is a cross-cutting Agentic AI Foundation initiative establishing a canonical vocabulary and tracking ecosystem artifacts across open-source agent frameworks[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-440eb159591a-19b34556]. Co-chaired by Junjie Bu (Google) and Gala Malbasic (Bloomberg), the workstream coordinates terminology proposals directly with specialized AAIF working groups through nominated Domain Editors[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-60f28464dd94-f4687b4a].

# Architecture / Specification

## Governance and Merging Rule

The workstream manages term lifecycles and merge criteria via a structured delegate model:
- **Domain Editors**: Each working group nominates delegates who serve as Domain Editors. They submit candidate terms from their respective groups, draft formal definitions, and conduct peer reviews[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-60f28464dd94-f4687b4a]. The proposing working group retains ownership of term definitions.
- **Two-Editor Asynchronous Approval**: Definition pull requests can merge asynchronously once two Domain Editors approve the pull request and all review comments have been resolved[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-60f28464dd94-f4687b4a]. Synchronous group votes are reserved exclusively for contested terms.
- **Review Filtering**: A Ready for Review milestone isolates pull requests that have advanced through the internal workstream process to ensure reviewer bandwidth is not consumed by unvetted submissions[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-60f28464dd94-f4687b4a].

## Deliverables

- **Unified Agentic Taxonomy**: Formalized repository of cross-cutting and specialized definitions spanning [Agentic Commerce](agentic-commerce.md), [Security & Privacy](security-and-privacy.md), and [Workflows & Process Integration](workflows-and-process-integration.md)[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-60f28464dd94-f4687b4a].
- **Agentic AI Landscape**: Ecosystem catalog ([Agentic AI Landscape](../resources/agentic-ai-landscape.md)) tracking industry implementations, paused in September 2026 to prioritize terminology and await [Technical Committee](../governance/technical-committee.md) neutrality guidelines[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-60f28464dd94-f4687b4a].

# Lifecycle History

- **June 2026**: Charter approved, base schemas established, and web portal pipelines deployed[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-440eb159591a-19b34556].
- **July–August 2026**: Terminology triage syncs evaluated candidate batches, merging the first 13-term pull request[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-440eb159591a-19b34556].
- **September 2026**: Seven definitions merged across four pull requests (`Human curated`, `Meta-harness`, `Human in the loop`, `Trust`, `Identifier`, `Attestation`, `Delegation`), two-Domain-Editor asynchronous approval established, and Landscape contributions paused[^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-60f28464dd94-f4687b4a].
