# Overview
The Taxonomy & Landscape Workstream serves as the horizontal architectural bridge connecting all vertical Technical Working Groups (WGs) within the AAIF. Its mission is to curate and maintain a unified glossary of agentic AI terms (the Taxonomy) and plan a CNCF-style ecosystem market map (the Landscape) [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-b33563055168-07a3beac].

# Architecture / Specification
The workstream is currently focused exclusively on the Taxonomy; Landscape contributions are deferred until an accepted process is defined [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-pr-61].

Governance operates using a three-tiered model: Contributors, Domain Editors (WG Delegates), and Maintainers (Chairs). Changes require approval from at least two or three Domain Editors from different WGs to ensure cross-functional alignment [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-eca12c0a30e2-333c16a8].

# Lifecycle History
**Chairs:** Junjie Bu (Google) and Gala Malbasic (Bloomberg) [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-b33563055168-07a3beac].

Key success metrics include Citation Utility (term adoption), Vendor-Neutral Diversity (contribution equilibrium), Ecosystem Coverage (≥ 10 reviewed terms per WG), and Arbitration Velocity (conflict resolution speed) [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-a54ff182c7e8-35ff909a].
