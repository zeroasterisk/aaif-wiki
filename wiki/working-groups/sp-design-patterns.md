# Overview
This workstream curates reusable design patterns for secure and privacy-respecting agentic systems, intended to be adopted by providers and consumers [^evt-wg-security-and-privacy-file-b33563055168-a40a890b].

# Deliverables
The catalog has incorporated kill-switch design patterns, covering different types of kill switches and methods for incorporation. Memory-related patterns are planned for subsequent development [^evt-wg-security-and-privacy-file-5d17bf85b2af-7c215d2d].
