# Overview
The Identity and Trust Working Group (I&T) advances the Agentic AI Foundation's mission by establishing vendor-neutral, interoperable viewpoints, reference architectures, and standards for AI agent identity, attestation, authentication, delegation, authorization, and auditability [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-b33563055168-5c93d5a3]. Its scope covers discovery, authority chains, human consent evidence, and cross-domain trust boundary crossing for autonomous and semi-autonomous systems [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-b33563055168-5c93d5a3].

# Leadership and Governance
The working group chairs are Grant Miller (IBM) and Alper Dedeođlu (SAP SE), serving terms from March 1, 2026 through January 31, 2027 following appointment by Platinum and Gold working group members [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-b33563055168-5c93d5a3]. Public working group meetings alternate bi-weekly between 6:30 AM and 9:00 AM USA Pacific Time on Thursdays [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-b33563055168-5c93d5a3].

# Breakout Streams and Organization
Technical exploration is divided across three coordinated breakout streams [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-24e1b9b7651b-91f43f6f]:
- **Use Cases**: Grounds requirements in consumer and enterprise deployment scenarios (coordinated by Wei Chen) [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-24e1b9b7651b-91f43f6f].
- **Reference Architecture**: Formulates actors, components, and trust models for agent identity and delegated authority (coordinated by Brian Malone) [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-24e1b9b7651b-91f43f6f].
- **Taxonomy Coordination**: Harmonizes identity terminology with the cross-group `../initiatives/taxonomy-and-landscape.md` workstream (liaisons: Julianna Mealin and Leonardo Galesky) [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-24e1b9b7651b-91f43f6f].

# Related Work and Evaluated Specifications
The group actively tracks and evaluates relevant industry initiatives and standards [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-24e1b9b7651b-91f43f6f]:
- **Agentic Resource Discovery (ARD)**: Discovery protocols and federation mechanisms for agent capabilities [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-24e1b9b7651b-91f43f6f].
- **Workload and Agent Attestation**: SPIFFE/SPIRE workload attestation foundation and Uber's multi-hop token exchange and actor chains [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-24e1b9b7651b-91f43f6f].
- **NVIDIA Secure Agent Workspace**: Layered architectural design for agent delegation and policy enforcement [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-24e1b9b7651b-91f43f6f].
- **IETF AI Agent Auth**: Draft specifications for agent-level authentication and authorization [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-24e1b9b7651b-91f43f6f].
