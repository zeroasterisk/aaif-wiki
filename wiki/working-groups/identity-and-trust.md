# Overview
AAIF working group identifying and promoting open standards and reference architectures for AI agent identity, attestation, authentication, delegation, and authorization.

The Identity & Trust Working Group (I&T) focuses on ensuring agents can be discovered, prove who they are, who they act for, what they are allowed to do, and what human approvals occurred, enabling secure, privacy-preserving, and auditable agent interactions across systems and organizational boundaries. [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-b33563055168-5c93d5a3]

# Architecture / Specification
The work is organized into several breakout groups:
*   **Use Cases:** Grounds the work in enterprise and consumer scenarios and derives identity and trust requirements.
*   **Reference Architecture:** Describes the actors, components, trust relationships, and interoperability gaps needed to support agent identity and delegated authority.
*   **Taxonomy coordination:** Develops and socializes I&T terminology, coordinating with the cross-working-group Taxonomy & Landscape Workstream. [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-24e1b9b7651b-91f43f6f]
