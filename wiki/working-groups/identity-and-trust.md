# Overview
The Identity and Trust Working Group (I&T) is an Agentic AI Foundation working group advancing vendor-neutral, interoperable standards and viewpoints for AI agent identity, attestation, authentication, delegation, and authorization [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-b33563055168-5c93d5a3]. Its mission is to enable agents to be discovered across platforms, verify their provenance and human authority, operate securely across organizational boundaries, and produce auditable traces of human approval [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-b33563055168-5c93d5a3] [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-24e1b9b7651b-91f43f6f].

# Architecture / Specification
The working group structures its activities across three specialized breakout groups that coordinate with the main working group cadence [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-24e1b9b7651b-91f43f6f]:
- **Use Cases Breakout** (coordinated by Wei Chen): Grounds requirements in enterprise and consumer agent scenarios and authority flows [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-24e1b9b7651b-91f43f6f].
- **Reference Architecture Breakout** (coordinated by Brian Malone): Maps actors, trust domains, component relationships, and delegation gaps [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-24e1b9b7651b-91f43f6f].
- **Taxonomy Coordination** (liaisons Julianna Mealin and Leonardo Galesky): Harmonizes identity terminology with the cross-working-group [Taxonomy & Landscape Workstream](../working-groups/taxonomy-and-landscape.md) [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-24e1b9b7651b-91f43f6f].

The group evaluates external technical foundations and reference works including SPIFFE/SPIRE workload attestation, the Agentic Resource Discovery (ARD) specification, Uber's agent identity and token exchange architecture, NVIDIA's Secure Agent Workspace reference design, and IETF AI agent authentication and authorization internet drafts [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-24e1b9b7651b-91f43f6f].

# Governance and Operations
- **Leadership**: Chaired by Grant Miller (IBM) and co-chaired by Alper Dedeoğlu (SAP SE) for the March 1, 2026 – January 31, 2027 term [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-b33563055168-5c93d5a3].
- **Cadence**: Public meetings occur every Thursday, alternating weekly between 6:30 AM PT and 9:00 AM PT [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-b33563055168-5c93d5a3].
- **Tracking and Agendas**: Meeting notes, AI summaries, and recordings are archived via LFX and Google Docs, while unallocated proposals and presentation topics are tracked in the outstanding agenda queue [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-b33563055168-5c93d5a3] [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-c743b929b22e-3767aad0].
- **Collaboration**: Public communication is hosted on the `wg-identity-trust` mailing list and dedicated AAIF Discord channels under Linux Foundation antitrust and neutrality commitments [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-b33563055168-5c93d5a3].
