# Overview

The Workflows and Process Integration Working Group (WG) advances the AAIF mission by establishing a shared workflow model and common terminology for agentic AI systems. It extends open specifications and projects, producing interoperability guidelines and design patterns that allow agent workflows to operate across frameworks, tools, and execution environments [^evt-wg-workflows-and-process-integration-file-b33563055168-a6ab4b87].

# Leadership and Operations

The current Chairs are Yaron Schneider (Diagrid) and Adam Seligman (Workato). They were appointed by the Platinum and Gold members of the working group, with a term running from March 1, 2026, to January 31, 2027 [^evt-wg-workflows-and-process-integration-file-b33563055168-a6ab4b87].

The WG meets biweekly on Thursdays at 8:00 AM PT [^evt-wg-workflows-and-process-integration-file-b33563055168-a6ab4b87].
