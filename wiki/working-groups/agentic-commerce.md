# Overview

The Agentic Commerce Working Group evaluates protocols and architectural standards for autonomous purchasing, transaction delegation, identity verification, and multi-jurisdictional compliance [^evt-wg-agentic-commerce-issue-8].

# Architecture / Specification

### Capability Mapping Dimensions

Recent workstream analysis identifies key requirements for protocol evaluation:

- **Buyer Legal Capacity & Age Verification**: Evaluating whether commerce protocols convey legal capacity attributes of the delegating principal (e.g., age verification and minor delegation status across jurisdictions) [^evt-wg-agentic-commerce-issue-8].
- **Multi-Layered Retention Requirements**: Managing transaction and interaction logs across three distinct regulatory tiers: AI high-risk audit obligations, personal data storage limits, and anti-money laundering / payments regulations (e.g., EU AMLD, US BSA, Regulation E) [^evt-wg-agentic-commerce-issue-8].
