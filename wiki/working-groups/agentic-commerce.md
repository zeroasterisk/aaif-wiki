# Overview
The Agentic Commerce Working Group develops vendor-neutral capability maps, interoperability profiles, and reference architectures for autonomous and semi-autonomous commercial transactions conducted by AI agents.[^evt-wg-agentic-commerce-issue-8]

# Architecture / Specification
The group's capability mapping evaluates protocols across transaction lifecycle requirements, addressing specific multi-layer compliance and execution dimensions:[^evt-wg-agentic-commerce-issue-8]
- **Buyer Capacity and Verification**: Standardizing representations for legal capacity, age verification, and delegated authority to prevent void or voidable delegations across differing jurisdictions.[^evt-wg-agentic-commerce-issue-8]
- **Multi-Layer Record Retention**: Specifying data retention obligations across three regulatory tiers:[^evt-wg-agentic-commerce-issue-8]
  - *AI Regulatory Layer*: High-risk AI system record-keeping rules (e.g., EU AI Act Articles 19 and 26(6) 6-month retention).[^evt-wg-agentic-commerce-issue-8]
  - *Data Protection Layer*: Storage limitation ceilings under GDPR and related privacy frameworks.[^evt-wg-agentic-commerce-issue-8]
  - *Financial and AML Layer*: Statutory transaction log durations under anti-money-laundering directives, the US Bank Secrecy Act (5-year periods), and payment rules such as Regulation E (2-year periods).[^evt-wg-agentic-commerce-issue-8]

# Lifecycle History
Active discussions ongoing regarding protocol capability evaluation criteria for autonomous agent checkouts and multi-party retention responsibilities.[^evt-wg-agentic-commerce-issue-8]
