# Overview
The Evidence-Strength Labels proposal specifies a vendor-neutral classification vocabulary to assess the verification rigor of claims concerning agent identity, provenance, delegation, and consent [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-issue-5]. Rather than treating assertions as binary or embedding static strength claims within credentials, strength labels are defined strictly as verifier appraisal outputs that record verified depth [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-issue-5].

# Architecture / Specification
The specification separates evidence appraisal across two orthogonal axes and a distinct freshness dimension [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-issue-5]:

## Appraisal Output Discipline
- Labels represent findings generated by the relying party verifier based on executed checks, prohibiting verifiers from self-grading above verified depth [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-issue-5].
- When evidence contradicts an assertion, the appraisal fails closed rather than downgrading scores [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-issue-5].

## Axis A: Assertion Origin (Who Asserts)
1. **Declared**: Assertions made solely by the agent or operator without independent validation [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-issue-5].
2. **Platform-Attested**: Claims vouched for by the host runtime, hypervisor, or hosting infrastructure [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-issue-5].
3. **Corroborated**: Affirmations independently confirmed by an external third party, identity provider, or counterparty [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-issue-5].

## Axis B: Chain Construction (How the Chain is Built)
4. **Composed**: Multi-attestation verifiable claims binding separate identities, delegation hops, or consent records under a weakest-hop constraint [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-issue-5].
5. **Anchored**: Verifiable claims terminating in an independently verifiable trust anchor such as a hardware root of trust, signed mandate, or decentralized identifier (DID) document [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-issue-5].

## Freshness and Revocation Dimension
Revocation, freshness, and expiry checks operate as an orthogonal evaluation dimension rather than a fixed rank [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-issue-5]. Verifiers fail closed if revocation state cannot be verified and explicitly record unperformed checks [^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-issue-5].
