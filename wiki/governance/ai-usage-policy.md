# Overview
The AI Usage Policy is being developed to ensure quality and consistency across foundation documentation, particularly within the [Cross-Working Group Taxonomy](../specification/taxonomy.md). It mandates human oversight and judgment when utilizing AI tools for generating or reviewing content [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-a51b00ec455f-a054be90].

# Scope
This policy is intended to govern the use of AI in drafting definitions and related documentation, ensuring that human domain editors maintain final authority and responsibility for accuracy and alignment with standards [^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-a51b00ec455f-a054be90].
