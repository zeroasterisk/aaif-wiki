# Overview
The Case Study Guidelines outline the standards, formats, and review processes for Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) member organizations sharing real-world implementations of foundation technologies and standards[^evt-foundation-file-d803a4dfa3ec-79586e70].

# Strategic Focus and Structure
Case studies must demonstrate measurable business impact resulting from the adoption of open agentic AI standards such as the Model Context Protocol (MCP), goose, or AGENTS.md[^evt-foundation-file-d803a4dfa3ec-79586e70].

Core evaluation themes include:
- **Agentic Maturity**: Progressing from basic conversational interfaces to autonomous, goal-directed agent systems[^evt-foundation-file-d803a4dfa3ec-79586e70].
- **Interoperability**: Implementing seamless integrations across heterogeneous models, environments, and tooling via open specifications[^evt-foundation-file-d803a4dfa3ec-79586e70].
- **Measurable Acceleration**: Documenting quantitative benefits such as reduced resolution time, cost reductions, or higher operational throughput[^evt-foundation-file-d803a4dfa3ec-79586e70].

# Submission Formats
Members can participate via two primary mechanisms[^evt-foundation-file-d803a4dfa3ec-79586e70]:
1. **Written Case Studies**: Structured narratives covering organizational context, technical and business challenges, solution architecture, and verified business outcomes.
2. **Webinar Case Studies**: Recorded 20–30 minute presentations with interactive Q&A hosted across foundation channels.
