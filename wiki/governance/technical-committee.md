# Overview
The Technical Committee (TC) serves as the primary technical governing body of the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF), an initiative organized as a directed fund of The Linux Foundation[^evt-technical-committee-file-29954f63e09f-b0f27f04]. The TC assists the Governing Board (GB) by evaluating technical project proposals, establishing and overseeing working groups, and enforcing the foundation's project lifecycle policy[^evt-technical-committee-file-29954f63e09f-b0f27f04].

# Operations and Governance
Adopted on March 3, 2026, the TC Charter outlines the committee's scope, voting mechanics, and operational procedures[^evt-technical-committee-file-29954f63e09f-b0f27f04].

### Composition and Leadership
Each Platinum Member organization of the AAIF designates one voting representative to the TC[^evt-technical-committee-file-29954f63e09f-b0f27f04]. ### Core Responsibilities
Under its charter, the TC is tasked with[^evt-technical-committee-file-29954f63e09f-b0f27f04]:
- Establishing, managing, and archiving working groups across domain areas relevant to AAIF's scope.
- Coordinating technical priorities, shared vision, and cross-project integration between Technical Projects.
- Recommending projects to the Governing Board for acceptance and designating stage progressions under the [Project Lifecycle Policy](../policies/project-lifecycle-policy.md).
- Recommending resource allocation and mentorship priorities to support technical initiatives.
- Maintaining and applying technical policies subject to Governing Board approval.

### Decision Making and Voting
While operating primarily on consensus, formal voting proceeds on a one-vote-per-voting-member basis[^evt-technical-committee-file-29954f63e09f-b0f27f04]:
- **Quorum**: Requires at least 50% of voting TC members present to take formal binding action[^evt-technical-committee-file-29954f63e09f-b0f27f04].
- **Lazy Consensus**: Proposals achieve approval through a 7-day silent review window if no formal objections are filed[^evt-technical-committee-file-29954f63e09f-b0f27f04]. If an objection is lodged, resolution requires an electronic vote decided by a majority of all TC voting members[^evt-technical-committee-file-29954f63e09f-b0f27f04].
- **Escalation**: Unresolvable votes may be referred to the Governing Board for final resolution[^evt-technical-committee-file-29954f63e09f-b0f27f04].
- **Charter Amendments**: Require approval by a two-thirds majority of the TC and final ratification by the Governing Board[^evt-technical-committee-file-29954f63e09f-b0f27f04].

# Hosted Projects
The TC oversees technical projects admitted under the lifecycle model, including foundational efforts such as[^evt-technical-committee-file-b33563055168-8f2953e0]:
- **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** (Impact Stage): An open standard protocol facilitating client-tool and data source integration for LLM-powered applications.
- **goose** (Impact Stage): An open-source, extensible local-first agent framework supporting automated tool execution, multi-turn file edits, and testing across foundation models.
