# Overview

The Technical Committee (TC) is the technical governing body of the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF), responsible for overseeing technical standards, best practices, working groups, and technical vision across the foundation.[^evt-technical-committee-file-b33563055168-688b7092]

# Architecture / Specification

## Membership and Governance

Founding TC members were appointed by the AAIF Governing Board for a term running from March 1, 2026 through December 31, 2026.[^evt-technical-committee-file-b33563055168-688b7092] Leadership and committee membership includes:

- **David Soria Parra** (Anthropic) - TC Chair
- **Caitie McCaffrey** (Microsoft) - TC Co-Chair
- **Alan Blount** (Google)
- **Bradley Axen** (Block)
- **James Ward** (Amazon Web Services)
- **Nick Cooper** (OpenAI)
- **Sambhav Kothari** (Bloomberg)
- **Steve Faulkner** (Cloudflare)

## Foundation Projects

The TC oversees open-source and specification initiatives across lifecycle stages defined in the [Project Lifecycle Policy](../policies/project-lifecycle-policy.md). Founding Impact Stage projects stewarded under the TC include the Model Context Protocol (MCP), the goose agent harness, and AGENTS.MD.[^evt-technical-committee-file-b33563055168-688b7092]
