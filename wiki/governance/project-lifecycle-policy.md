# Overview

The Project Lifecycle Policy establishes the intake requirements, review criteria, and formal stages for technical projects hosted within the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF)[^evt-technical-committee-file-8f732085cd4f-49330cd0]. Managed by the [Technical Committee](../governance/technical-committee.md), it governs both external open-source projects seeking AAIF adoption and new initiatives formed directly within the foundation.

# Specification

## Proposal Process

Projects are submitted through the AAIF project proposals repository. Proposals require defined metadata, including project description, mission alignment, Technical Committee sponsorship, OSI-approved permissive licensing, release automation mechanics, public issue tracking, maintainer leadership structures, and documented governance practices[^evt-technical-committee-file-8f732085cd4f-49330cd0].

## Lifecycle Stages

Projects progress through four distinct stages based on maturity, governance, and ecosystem adoption[^evt-technical-committee-file-8f732085cd4f-49330cd0]:

- **Sandbox Stage**: Early-stage entry point for experimental or nascent projects aligning with AAIF scope.
- **Growth Stage**: Maturing projects demonstrating sustained adoption, active maintainer bases, and functional governance.
- **Impact Stage**: Production-grade, high-impact projects serving as core infrastructure or standards for the agentic ecosystem.
- **Emeritus Stage**: Inactive or retired projects that have completed their lifecycle and transition out of active maintenance.

# Lifecycle History

Evaluations and stage promotions are conducted via an Annual Review Process supervised by the Technical Committee[^evt-technical-committee-file-8f732085cd4f-49330cd0].
