# Overview
The AAIF Project Lifecycle Policy defines the official progression stages, entry criteria, and review processes for technical projects governed under the Agentic AI Foundation [^evt-technical-committee-pr-9]. It establishes transparent governance pathways for onboarding emerging projects into Sandbox, advancing mature projects to Growth and Impact stages, and archiving inactive efforts under Emeritus status [^evt-technical-committee-pr-9].

# Architecture / Specification
The project lifecycle defines four distinct operational stages [^evt-technical-committee-pr-9]:
- **Sandbox**: Entry stage for early-stage initiatives, experimental tools, and novel specifications seeking neutral collaboration and foundation incubation.
- **Growth**: Stage for active projects demonstrating multi-organization adoption, sustained contributor diversity, structured release management, and formal security policies.
- **Impact**: Highest maturity tier for mission-critical, widely deployed standards and runtimes demonstrating significant ecosystem governance and long-term sustainability.
- **Emeritus**: Stage for retired or deprecated projects transitioning out of active development.

Transitions between stages require formal Technical Committee review and approval according to established voting gates [^evt-technical-committee-pr-9].
