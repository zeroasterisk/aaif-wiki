# Overview

The Working Group Lifecycle Policy governs the formation, ongoing operations, oversight reporting, and dissolution of AAIF working groups and work streams.[^evt-wg-agentic-commerce-file-9677055e316b-dc950981]

# Architecture / Specification

## Monthly Reporting Process

Working groups and work streams submit structured monthly progress reports to keep the [Technical Committee](technical-committee.md) updated asynchronously:[^evt-wg-agentic-commerce-file-9677055e316b-dc950981][^evt-ws-taxonomy-landscape-file-9677055e316b-9ddc8f32]

- **Filing**: Reports are created in `reporting/YYYY-MM-report.md` via a pull request titled `[Report] <WG name> – <Month YYYY>`.[^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-9677055e316b-05c3ca6e]
- **Deadline**: Reports are due by the last business day of the reporting month.[^evt-wg-identity-and-trust-file-9677055e316b-2e851e23]
- **Review Window**: The TC review window closes 7 calendar days after submission. Reports are merged upon TC sign-off or when the review window closes, whichever occurs first.[^evt-wg-observability-and-traceability-file-9677055e316b-93d6f75a]
- **Standard Contents**: Reports contain a summary, progress against objectives, blockers and risks, decisions needed from the TC, next month's focus, and relevant metrics or links.[^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-9677055e316b-05c3ca6e]

# Lifecycle History

- Formalized monthly reporting workflow across all active working groups and work streams.[^evt-wg-governance-risk-and-regulatory-file-9677055e316b-05c3ca6e]
