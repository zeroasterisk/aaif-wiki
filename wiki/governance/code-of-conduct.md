# Overview
The Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) Code of Conduct establishes community standards and enforcement procedures to foster an inclusive, welcoming, and safe environment across all foundation activities [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588].

# Architecture / Specification
The Code of Conduct governs interactions within the foundation's official operations [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588]:

- **Scope**: Covers Governing Board, [Technical Committee](./technical-committee.md), Working Group, and Outreach Committee meetings, as well as broader foundation channels [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588].
- **Events**: Foundation-produced events operate under the Linux Foundation Events Code of Conduct in conjunction with this policy [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588].
- **Technical Projects**: Technical projects under the AAIF umbrella maintain and enforce individual project-level codes of conduct [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588].
- **Behavioral Standards**: Requires inclusive language, respect for diverse viewpoints, and constructive collaboration while strictly prohibiting harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and non-consensual disclosure of private information [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588].
