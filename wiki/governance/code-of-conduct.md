# Overview

The Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) Code of Conduct establishes guidelines for fostering an inclusive, welcoming, and safe environment across all Foundation activities, including technical projects, events, and communication channels [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588].

The Code applies to Governing Board, Technical Committee, Working Group, and Outreach Committee meetings, as well as any other AAIF activity not covered by a specific technical project's code of conduct. Events produced with professional staff are governed by the LF Events Code of Conduct in conjunction with this document. Technical projects under the AAIF umbrella maintain their own codes of conduct [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588].

# Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to use welcoming and inclusive language, respect differing viewpoints, accept constructive criticism, prioritize community benefit, show empathy, and be professional and responsible in all interactions [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588].

# Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behaviors include harassment and discrimination (verbal, physical, visual, sexual harassment, stalking), inappropriate communication (sexualized language/imagery, violent language, trolling, personal attacks), and publishing private information without permission [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588].

# Enforcement

Violations can be reported to the designated contacts. Consequences for violations range from warnings to temporary or permanent exclusion from the community [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588].
