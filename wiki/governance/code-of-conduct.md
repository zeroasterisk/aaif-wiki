# Overview
The Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) Code of Conduct establishes community standards and rules of engagement across all Foundation activities [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588]. The Foundation pledges to foster an inclusive, welcoming, and harassment-free environment for all participants regardless of personal background or identity [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588].

# Architecture / Specification
## Scope
The Code of Conduct applies across AAIF governance and community interactions [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588]:
- Governing Board meetings
- Technical Committee meetings (see [Technical Committee](technical-committee.md))
- Working Group meetings
- Outreach Committee and other AAIF committee meetings
- General Foundation communications, channels, and social media activities

## Inter-Policy Relationships
- **Foundation Events**: Events produced by the AAIF with professional events staff are governed by the Linux Foundation Events Code of Conduct in conjunction with this Code of Conduct [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588].
- **Umbrella Technical Projects**: Technical projects supported by the AAIF operate as distinct projects and maintain their own codes of conduct [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588].

## Behavioral Standards
- **Expected Behavior**: Community members must use welcoming language, respect alternative perspectives, accept constructive feedback, prioritize community benefit, and collaborate responsibly [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588].
- **Unacceptable Behavior**: Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, sustained disruption, sexualized or threatening language, personal attacks, and unauthorized sharing of private information are prohibited [^evt-foundation-file-ffdbe3a1e7ee-0ec44588].
