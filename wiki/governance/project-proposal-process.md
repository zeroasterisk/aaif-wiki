# Overview

The Project Proposal Process defines the formal intake, evaluation, and approval lifecycle for open source software seeking official hosting under the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) [^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1]. Open source projects apply for placement within the AAIF maturity levels—Sandbox, Growth, or Impact—via structured proposals reviewed by the Technical Committee and Governing Board [^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1].

# Architecture / Specification

## Intake and Submission

Proposals are submitted via the `aaif/project-proposals` intake repository using standardized templates that capture [^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1]:
- Project name, origin, scope, and technical description [^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1].
- Alignment with the AAIF mission and ecosystem architecture [^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1].
- Relationship and interoperability with existing AAIF projects and working groups [^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1].
- Project maturity stage request (Sandbox, Growth, or Impact) [^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1].

## Approval Gates and Legal Execution

Admission into the foundation requires strict dual-body approval and legal execution sequence [^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1]:

1. **Technical Committee (TC) Evaluation**: The [Technical Committee](technical-committee.md) assesses architectural fit, technical maturity, community engagement, and ecosystem overlap [^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1].
2. **Contribution Agreement**: A formal Contribution Agreement is generated and must be signed by the project maintainers/owners before the Governing Board can complete its vote [^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1].
3. **Governing Board (GB) Vote**: The Governing Board votes on the intake following successful TC recommendation and agreement signing [^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1].
4. **Countersignature**: The Linux Foundation countersigns the Contribution Agreement only after a successful GB vote [^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1].

> Projects are legally prohibited from representing themselves as official AAIF projects or 'donated' to AAIF until both TC and GB votes have passed and the Contribution Agreement has been fully executed [^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1].
