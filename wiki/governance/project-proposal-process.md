# Overview

The AAIF Project Proposal Process establishes the formal submission and evaluation pipeline for external open source projects seeking foundation hosting under Sandbox, Growth, or Impact project maturity tiers [^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1]. The workflow enforces rigorous governance vetting by both the [Technical Committee](./technical-committee.md) (TC) and the Governing Board (GB), paired with mandatory Linux Foundation legal agreements [^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1].

# Architecture / Specification

## Intake and Submission Requirements

Proposals are submitted through standardized issue intake templates in `aaif/project-proposals` [^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1]. Applications must detail:
- **Maturity Alignment:** Demonstrating strong community momentum filling critical gaps for Sandbox stage, or broad adoption and maturity for Growth/Impact stages [^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1].
- **Mission & Ecosystem Alignment:** Explicit documentation of how the project integrates with existing AAIF reference architectures and working groups [^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1].
- **Maintainer & Contact Disclosure:** Identifying primary maintainers and legal contacts required for foundation agreements [^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1].

## Evaluation and Approval Gates

Project onboarding requires completing four consecutive gates [^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1]:
1. **Technical Committee Evaluation:** The TC reviews technical readiness, architecture neutrality, and alignment [^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1].
2. **Contribution Agreement Execution:** Maintainers and contributing organizations must sign an official Contribution Agreement. Projects cannot represent themselves as donated or contributed until executed [^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1].
3. **Governing Board Vote:** The GB votes on formal acceptance following successful agreement transmission [^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1].
4. **Linux Foundation Countersignature:** The Linux Foundation countersigns the agreement following affirmative TC and GB approvals [^evt-project-proposals-file-6c7de919a622-a1bc6ba1].
