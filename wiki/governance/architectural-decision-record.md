# Overview

The Architectural Decision Record (ADR) template is used to document significant architectural choices made by the working group. It ensures transparency and provides historical context for why a particular path was chosen over alternatives [^evt-wg-workflows-and-process-integration-file-3a311f3edf5a-27d0e407].

# Specification

An ADR must capture:

1.  **Context**: What prompted the decision, including the prior state and the ambiguity or constraint requiring resolution.
2.  **Decision**: The resolution itself.
3.  **Rationale**: The reasoning behind the decision.
4.  **Consequences**: What changes if the decision is adopted, including follow-on work and known tradeoffs.
5.  **Alternatives Considered**: The credible alternatives and why they were not selected [^evt-wg-workflows-and-process-integration-file-3a311f3edf5a-27d0e407].

Decisions are tracked by status (Proposed, Accepted, Rejected, Superseded) and include links to relevant review discussions or pull requests.
