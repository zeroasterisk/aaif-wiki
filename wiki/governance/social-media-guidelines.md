# Overview

The Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) social media content strategy, account access, and brand compliance are led by the Linux Foundation staff to ensure neutral, professional, and consistent ecosystem communication[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637]. Social platforms serve to broadcast official Foundation-wide updates, milestones, releases, events, and initiatives across the open source AI ecosystem[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637].

# Architecture / Specification

### Content Baselines
AAIF social channels maintain a neutral, inclusive, and professional tone aligned with foundational brand values[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637] (see [../governance/brand-foundation.md](brand-foundation.md)):
- **Relevance**: All posts, shares, and replies must focus on open-source agentic AI, community events, or learning resources[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637].
- **No Unsolicited Promotions**: Generic self-promotions, advertisements, product announcements, and sponsored content are prohibited[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637].
- **Attribution**: Proper attribution must be given for shared code or ideas while respecting open source licenses[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637].
- **Platform Alignment**: Content is tailored across platforms, utilizing X for concise announcements, LinkedIn for professional case studies and technical discussions, and Bluesky for conversational community engagement[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637].

### Content Submission and Amplification
Community members and organizations can request social amplification through two designated channels[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637]:
1. **Direct Reposts**: Submitted through the designated AAIF Discord social amplification channel[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637]. Reposts are limited to official project releases, working group announcements, official events, and community projects explicitly implementing AAIF standards[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637].
2. **Organic AAIF Posts**: Submitted via the AAIF Content Intake Form for structured review and scheduling by Foundation staff[^evt-foundation-file-135a8fc4e3c5-bc2ab637].
