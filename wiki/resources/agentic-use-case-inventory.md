# Overview
The Agentic Use Case Inventory documents real-world, deployed agentic applications to identify reusable architectural patterns from empirical industry deployments [^evt-wg-workflows-and-process-integration-pr-46]. Developed by the Critical Use Cases workstream under the [Workflows and Process Integration Working Group](../working-groups/workflows-and-process-integration.md), the inventory functions as an empirical sourcing and classification catalog rather than a prioritization exercise [^evt-wg-workflows-and-process-integration-pr-46].

# Architecture / Specification
Each entry in the inventory is mapped according to the dimensional taxonomy defined in [agentic-use-case-classification](../resources/agentic-use-case-classification.md) and governed by explicit evidentiary standards [^evt-wg-workflows-and-process-integration-pr-46]:
- **Evidentiary Standard**: Every notes entry must cite a verifiable vendor product page or primary announcement demonstrating confirmed production deployment [^evt-wg-workflows-and-process-integration-pr-46].
- **Citation Thresholds**: A minimum of one verified production citation is required for inclusion, with a target benchmark of three verified citations per use case [^evt-wg-workflows-and-process-integration-pr-46].
- **Source Tiers**: Citations are categorized into preferred (primary technical documentation, architecture whitepapers, vendor product announcements of live deployments), acceptable (reputable secondary industry reporting with confirmed deployment details), and non-acceptable tiers (speculative previews, unconfirmed rumors, or unverified claims) [^evt-wg-workflows-and-process-integration-pr-46].
- **Review Lifecycle**: Classification schemas and evidentiary standards advance through formal working group votes, while row-level citations and notes undergo ongoing community verification [^evt-wg-workflows-and-process-integration-pr-46].
