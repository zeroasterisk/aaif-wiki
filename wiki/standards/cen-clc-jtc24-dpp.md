# Overview
The CEN/CENELEC JTC 24 Digital Product Passport standards (EN 18216 to EN 18246) are horizontal technical standards stewarded by the joint technical committee of CEN and CENELEC [^evt-ext-frmoretto-agentic-standards-map-85d122-file-f66bee5cf433-00f4b591]. They establish standardized data architectures, unique identification rules, and searchability interfaces for Digital Product Passports under the European Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation [^evt-ext-frmoretto-agentic-standards-map-85d122-file-f66bee5cf433-00f4b591].

Within the agentic domain, these horizontal specifications govern how physical products are identified and resolved by automated systems, interfacing with the GS1 conformant resolver (`../standards/gs1-conformant-resolver.md`).

# Architecture / Specification
The JTC 24 architecture comprises eight horizontal standards [^evt-ext-frmoretto-agentic-standards-map-85d122-file-f66bee5cf433-00f4b591]:
- **Data Exchange Protocols (EN 18216)**: Rules governing structured transmission of passport data between systems [^evt-ext-frmoretto-agentic-standards-map-85d122-file-f66bee5cf433-00f4b591].
- **Unique Identifiers (EN 18219)**: Formats and syntax rules for globally unique product identity resolution [^evt-ext-frmoretto-agentic-standards-map-85d122-file-f66bee5cf433-00f4b591].
- **Data Carriers (EN 18220)**: Requirements for physical media (such as QR codes or RFID) holding identifiers [^evt-ext-frmoretto-agentic-standards-map-85d122-file-f66bee5cf433-00f4b591].
- **Data Storage (EN 18221)**: Storage models for distributed and centralized product record persistence [^evt-ext-frmoretto-agentic-standards-map-85d122-file-f66bee5cf433-00f4b591].
- **APIs for Lifecycle Management and Searchability (EN 18222)**: Standardized APIs enabling external agents and platforms to query and search product records across life cycles [^evt-ext-frmoretto-agentic-standards-map-85d122-file-f66bee5cf433-00f4b591].
- **System Interoperability (EN 18223)**: Cross-system integration and semantic mapping protocols [^evt-ext-frmoretto-agentic-standards-map-85d122-file-f66bee5cf433-00f4b591].
- **Access Rights and Security (EN 18239)**: Security models controlling public, commercial, and regulatory access [^evt-ext-frmoretto-agentic-standards-map-85d122-file-f66bee5cf433-00f4b591].
- **Data Authentication and Integrity (EN 18246)**: Integrity verification and cryptographic proof mechanisms [^evt-ext-frmoretto-agentic-standards-map-85d122-file-f66bee5cf433-00f4b591].

The scope is restricted to horizontal requirements; product-specific parameters are delegated to distinct product-specific Technical Committees [^evt-ext-frmoretto-agentic-standards-map-85d122-file-f66bee5cf433-00f4b591].
