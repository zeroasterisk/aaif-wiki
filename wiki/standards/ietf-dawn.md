# Overview
Discovery of Agents With Names (DAWN) is a proposed Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF) working group; its draft charter is in external review and the IESG has not yet made a determination [^evt-ext-frmoretto-agentic-standards-map-85d122-file-cfafda5329df-254665a0]. The draft charter proposes to standardize mechanisms for discovering public properties of AI resources, such as agent reachability, supported communication protocols, and service endpoints across local network environments and the public Internet [^evt-ext-frmoretto-agentic-standards-map-85d122-file-cfafda5329df-254665a0].

# Architecture / Specification
- **Deployment Contexts**: The draft charter outlines discovery models for both local networks (e.g., discovering co-located agents within a local environment) and cooperating organizations over the public Internet [^evt-ext-frmoretto-agentic-standards-map-85d122-file-cfafda5329df-254665a0].
- **Minimum Discovery Information**: Focuses on defining the minimum discovery schema required to establish contact, including agent type, transport reachability, communication protocols, and references to external discovery services like catalogs [^evt-ext-frmoretto-agentic-standards-map-85d122-file-cfafda5329df-254665a0].
- **Scope Boundaries**: Full capability catalogs, post-discovery agent-to-agent communication, broad Internet indexing/crawling, and agent identity management are excluded from the initial charter scope [^evt-ext-frmoretto-agentic-standards-map-85d122-file-cfafda5329df-254665a0].
